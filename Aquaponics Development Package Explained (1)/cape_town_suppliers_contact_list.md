## MONEY-SAVING TIPS

### 1. BULK BUYING
- Order seeds in larger quantities for better prices
- Buy nutrients in 5L containers instead of 1L
- Purchase trays in bulk (50+ at a time)

### 2. TIMING YOUR PURCHASES
- Shop during end-of-season sales
- Buy equipment during winter when demand is lower
- Check for online specials before visiting stores

### 3. BUILDING RELATIONSHIPS
- Introduce yourself as a new business owner
- Ask about business discounts
- Request to be notified of sales and specials
