# Homemade Nutrient Solutions Research for Scarcity Preparedness

## Critical Finding: Nitrate-Rich Compost Tea

### Source
Science in Hydroponics - "Making a nitrate rich compost tea for organic hydroponics"
https://scienceinhydroponics.com/2021/04/making-a-nitrate-rich-compost-tea-for-organic-hydroponics.html

### The Problem with Organic Nutrients
- Most organic nitrogen is in ammonium or protein form
- Plants prefer nitrate form
- Mined sodium nitrate contains too much sodium (harmful)
- Synthetic nitrates cannot be used in organic systems

### The Solution: Bacterial Conversion Process

**Key Ingredients:**
1. **Corn Steep Liquor (CSL)** - 1g/L (protein/nitrogen source)
2. **Bark Compost** - 0.5g/L (source of saprophytic bacteria)
3. **Aeration** - continuous oxygen supply
4. **Time** - 12 days for full conversion

**Process:**
- Bacteria in bark compost convert protein/ammonium nitrogen into nitrate
- After 12 days: 450-600 ppm nitrate achieved (100-130 ppm N as nitrate)
- Solution is low in sodium, high in plant-available nitrogen

**For 5 Gallons (19L):**
- 19g Corn Steep Liquor
- 9.5g Bark Compost
- Aerate continuously for 12 days

### Research Results
- Successfully grew tomatoes comparable to chemical fertilizers
- Still requires Ca and Mg supplementation
- NPK values can be adjusted by changing CSL amount

## Alternative: Fish Emulsion
- Also works but very smelly
- Not recommended for large-scale use
- CSL is preferred

## Bulk Materials to Stockpile NOW

### For Homemade Nutrients:

**1. Corn Steep Liquor (CSL)**
- Primary nitrogen source
- Buy in bulk (25kg bags or drums)
- Long shelf life when dry
- Source: Agricultural suppliers, brewing suppliers

**2. Bark Compost**
- Source of beneficial bacteria
- Can be made at home from tree bark
- Stockpile or learn to produce

**3. Calcium Sources (for Ca supplementation):**
- **Eggshells** - grind into powder, dissolve in vinegar
- **Bone meal** - long-term calcium source
- **Gypsum (Calcium Sulfate)** - mineral source
- **Oyster shells** - grind into powder

**4. Magnesium Sources:**
- **Epsom salts (Magnesium Sulfate)** - buy in bulk (25kg bags)
- Very stable, long shelf life
- Essential for chlorophyll production

**5. Potassium Sources:**
- **Wood ash** - from burning hardwood (free!)
- **Banana peels** - dry and grind into powder
- **Kelp meal** - seaweed-based, buy in bulk

**6. Phosphorus Sources:**
- **Bone meal** - excellent P source
- **Rock phosphate** - long-term source
- **Fish bone meal** - if available

**7. Micronutrients:**
- **Kelp meal** - contains full spectrum of micronutrients
- **Rock dust** - mineral source
- **Azomite** - volcanic rock dust with 70+ minerals

## Additional Homemade Nutrient Methods

### Bokashi Tea
- Fermented organic matter
- Use 1oz per gallon of water
- Contains living microbes and nutrients

### Worm Leachate (Worm Tea)
- Liquid that drains from worm farm
- Rich in nutrients and beneficial bacteria
- Dilute 1:10 with water

### Compost Tea (General)
- Soak quality compost in aerated water
- 24-48 hours brewing time
- 1 cup concentrate per gallon water

### Kitchen Waste Fertilizer
- Banana peels (potassium)
- Potato peels (potassium)
- Eggshells (calcium)
- Coffee grounds (nitrogen)
- Ferment in water for 2-4 weeks

### Wood Ash Tea
- Sprinkle wood ash in water
- Provides potassium and calcium
- Use sparingly (raises pH)

## pH Management Without Chemicals

### To Lower pH (Make More Acidic):
- Vinegar (acetic acid)
- Citric acid (from lemons/limes)
- Sulfur (elemental)

### To Raise pH (Make More Alkaline):
- Wood ash
- Crushed eggshells
- Baking soda (short term)

## Emergency Backup: Urine as Fertilizer

**Human urine is an excellent nitrogen source:**
- Sterile when fresh
- High in nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium
- Dilute 1:10 with water
- Use immediately
- Free and renewable

**Caution:** Only use if no medications are being taken
