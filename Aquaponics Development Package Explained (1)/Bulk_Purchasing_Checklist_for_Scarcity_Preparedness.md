
# Bulk Purchasing Checklist & Timeline for Scarcity Preparedness

**Author:** Manus AI

**Purpose:** To provide a detailed, prioritized, and time-sensitive shopping list for creating a 5-10 year strategic reserve of non-renewable homesteading supplies before a period of extreme scarcity.

## The Principle: Stockpile What You Cannot Make

This is not a normal shopping list. This is a strategic acquisition plan. The goal is to front-load your investment and create a buffer that will allow you to build your resilient homestead without relying on fragile supply chains. Every item on this list has been chosen because it is either impossible or extremely difficult to produce on a small homestead.

**This phase is the most important and most urgent. It must be completed within the next 60 days.**

## WHERE TO BUY IN CAPE TOWN & SOUTH AFRICA

*   **Seeds:**
    *   **Heirloom:** Living Seeds (online), Seeds for Africa (online), local seed swap groups.
    *   **Fodder:** Your local agricultural co-op (Kaap Agri, Afgri).
    *   **Seed Vaults:** Takealot, specialized prepper websites.
*   **Minerals:**
    *   **Agricultural Co-ops:** For bulk Epsom salts, Gypsum, etc.
    *   **Hydroponic Shops:** For Kelp Meal, Azomite.
    *   **Brewing Supply Shops:** For Corn Steep Liquor.
*   **Infrastructure:**
    *   **Hydroponic Shops:** Pumps, tubing, fittings.
    *   **Builders Warehouse / Makro:** General hardware.
    *   **Plastics Manufacturers:** For bulk food-grade drums.
    *   **Solar Suppliers:** For panels, batteries, inverters (e.g., Sustainable.co.za, Sinetech).
*   **Tools & Supplies:**
    *   **Agrimark / Kaap Agri:** For quality farm tools.
    *   **Online Retailers:** For specialized items like scythes and Mylar bags.
    *   **Consol Glass / Packaging Suppliers:** For bulk jars and lids.

## Storage of Your Stockpile

*   **Seeds:** Must go into your **COOL, DARK, DRY, PROTECTED** seed bank immediately.
*   **Minerals:** Store in sealed, labeled buckets in a cool, dry garage or storeroom.
*   **Plastics & Tools:** Store out of direct sunlight to prevent UV degradation.
