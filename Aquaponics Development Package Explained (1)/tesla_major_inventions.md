# Tesla Research: Major Inventions and AC Motor Development

## AC Induction Motor and Polyphase System (1887-1888)

### Formation of Tesla Electric Company (1887)
- Met Alfred S. Brown (Western Union superintendent) and Charles Fletcher Peck (New York attorney)
- Formed Tesla Electric Company in April 1887
- Agreement: 1/3 profits to Tesla, 1/3 to Peck and Brown, 1/3 to fund development
- Set up laboratory at 89 Liberty Street in Manhattan

### The Revolutionary AC Motor (1887)
- Developed induction motor that ran on alternating current (AC)
- Used polyphase current which generated a rotating magnetic field
- Tesla claimed to have conceived the principle in 1882
- **Key Innovation**: Simple self-starting design that didn't need a commutator
- Avoided sparking and high maintenance of constantly servicing mechanical brushes
- Patented in May 1888 (U.S. patent 381,968)

### Public Demonstration and Recognition (1888)
- Physicist William Arnold Anthony and Electrical World magazine editor Thomas Commerford Martin arranged demonstration
- Tesla demonstrated AC motor on May 16, 1888 at American Institute of Electrical Engineers
- Westinghouse Electric & Manufacturing Company engineers reported to George Westinghouse
- Westinghouse recognized Tesla had viable AC motor and power system

### The Westinghouse Deal (July 1888)
- **Historic Licensing Agreement**: $60,000 in cash and stock plus $2.50 royalty per AC horsepower produced
- Westinghouse hired Tesla for one year at $2,000 ($70,000 in today's dollars) per month as consultant
- Tesla worked in Pittsburgh helping create alternating current system to power city's streetcars

### Financial Crisis and Royalty Release (1891)
- Financial panic of 1890 caused cash shortage for Westinghouse
- Westinghouse explained financial difficulties to Tesla
- Tesla agreed to release company from royalty payment clause
- Six years later, Westinghouse purchased Tesla's patent for lump sum of $216,000
- Part of patent-sharing agreement with General Electric

## Tesla Coil and High-Frequency Experiments (1889-1891)

### Tesla Coil Development (1889)
- Inspired by Heinrich Hertz's 1886-1888 experiments proving electromagnetic radiation
- Developed "oscillating transformer" with air gap instead of insulating material
- Iron core could be moved to different positions in or out of coil
- **Revolutionary Design**: Produced high-voltage, low-current, high-frequency alternating-current electricity
- Used resonant transformer circuit in later wireless power work

### Wireless Lighting Experiments (1890-1893)
- Experimented with transmitting power by inductive and capacitive coupling using high AC voltages
- Attempted wireless lighting system based on near-field inductive and capacitive coupling
- Public demonstrations lighting Geissler tubes and incandescent light bulbs wirelessly
- **Key Claim (1893)**: Told onlookers he could "conduct intelligible signals or perhaps even power to any distance without the use of wires" by conducting through the Earth

### Steam-Powered Oscillating Generator
- Introduced steam-powered reciprocating electricity generator at 1893 Columbian Exposition
- Steam forced into oscillator through ports, pushing piston that vibrated magnetic armature
- Produced alternating magnetic field that induced alternating electric current
- Never became feasible engineering solution but demonstrated innovative thinking

## World's Columbian Exposition (1893)
- Westinghouse won bid to light Exposition with alternating current
- Demonstrated safety, reliability, and efficiency of polyphase AC system
- Key event in "war of currents" against Edison's DC system
- Tesla's "Egg of Columbus" demonstration showed rotating magnetic field principle

## Niagara Falls Project (1893)
- Edward Dean Adams sought Tesla's advice on power transmission system
- Tesla recommended two-phase system as most reliable
- Westinghouse awarded contract for two-phase AC generating system
- General Electric built AC distribution system
- Established AC as standard for power generation and distribution



## Patent Progression Toward Wireless Power Transmission (1888-1900)

### Early AC Motor and Power System Patents (1888-1889)
- **U.S. Patent 381,968** (May 1, 1888): Electro-Magnetic Motor - First practical AC motor
- **U.S. Patent 382,280** (May 1, 1888): Electrical Transmission of Power - AC power distribution
- **U.S. Patent 382,281** (May 1, 1888): Electrical Transmission of Power - Polyphase system
- **U.S. Patent 416,194** (December 3, 1889): Electric Motor - Multiple energizing circuits
- **U.S. Patent 487,796** (December 13, 1892): System of Electrical Transmission of Power - Alternating current generator with independent armature-circuits

### High-Frequency and Tesla Coil Development (1891-1897)
- **U.S. Patent 454,622** (June 23, 1891): System of Electric Lighting - High-frequency electrical phenomena for "novel electrical phenomena, which require currents of higher frequency and potential"
- **U.S. Patent 455,069** (June 30, 1891): Electric Incandescent Lamp - Wireless lighting demonstration
- **U.S. Patent 462,418** (November 3, 1891): Method of and Apparatus for Electrical Conversion and Distribution - Converting energy for "novel electrical phenomena which require currents of higher frequency and potential"
- **U.S. Patent 512,340** (January 9, 1894): Coil for Electro-Magnets - Bifilar coil design crucial for wireless power
- **U.S. Patent 514,168** (February 6, 1894): Means for Generating Electric Currents - "Electrical energy discovered by Tesla" involving oscillatory discharges
- **U.S. Patent 568,176** (September 22, 1896): Apparatus for Producing Electrical Currents of High Frequency and Potential - Tesla coil technology
- **U.S. Patent 568,178** (September 22, 1896): Method of Regulating Apparatus for Producing Electric Currents of High Frequency - Cited by Tesla in "the True Wireless"
- **U.S. Patent 577,670** (February 23, 1897): Apparatus for Producing Electric Currents of High Frequency
- **U.S. Patent 583,953** (June 8, 1897): Apparatus for Producing Currents of High Frequency - Converting ordinary current into "high frequency and high potential"
- **U.S. Patent 593,138** (November 2, 1897): Electrical Transformer - "Novel form of transformer" for "transmission of electrical energy" with "higher potential for transmission than has ever been practically employed heretofore"

### Wireless Control and Communication (1898)
- **U.S. Patent 613,809** (July 1, 1898): Method of and Apparatus for Controlling Mechanism of Moving Vehicle or Vehicles - Tesla "Boat" patent for remote control using electromagnetic waves with "no intermediate wires, cables, or other form of electrical or mechanical connection"

### Wireless Power Transmission Patents (1900)
- **U.S. Patent 645,576** (March 20, 1900): **System of Transmission of Electrical Energy** - Tesla's key wireless power transmission patent
  - Filed September 2, 1897
  - Wireless power transmission scheme using tethered balloons at 30,000 feet altitude
  - Based on highly conductive atmospheric layer concept
  - Could "transmit intelligible messages to great distances"
  - Could "illuminate upper strata of the air" for nighttime lighting
  - Based on Crookes tube low-pressure conductivity phenomenon

- **U.S. Patent 649,621** (May 15, 1900): **Apparatus for Transmission of Electrical Energy**
  - Related to US645576
  - "Transmitting coil or conductor arranged and excited to cause currents or oscillation to propagate through conduction through the natural medium from one point to another remote point"
  - "Production of currents of very high potential"
  - "Transmitting station and receiving station"

- **U.S. Patent 655,838** (October 23, 1900): Method of Insulating Electric Conductors
  - Related to Faraday's hypothesis about freezing substances
  - Method for insulating transmission conductors
  - Improvements in insulation for high-voltage applications

- **U.S. Patent 685,012** (March 21, 1900): **Means for Increasing the Intensity of Electrical Oscillations**
  - Method for producing "great increase in the intensity and duration" of electrical oscillations
  - Essential for wireless power transmission efficiency

## Key Technological Progression

### Phase 1: AC Motor Foundation (1888-1889)
Tesla established the fundamental AC motor and polyphase power distribution system that revolutionized electrical power generation and transmission.

### Phase 2: High-Frequency Exploration (1891-1897)
Tesla developed high-frequency, high-voltage technologies including the Tesla coil, which enabled wireless lighting demonstrations and laid the groundwork for wireless power transmission.

### Phase 3: Wireless Communication (1898)
Tesla demonstrated wireless control of mechanical devices, proving that electromagnetic waves could transmit both power and information without wires.

### Phase 4: Wireless Power Transmission (1900)
Tesla patented comprehensive systems for transmitting electrical power wirelessly through the Earth's atmosphere and conductive layers, representing the culmination of his work toward "free energy" distribution.

## Scientific Basis for Wireless Power Claims

### Atmospheric Conductivity Theory
Tesla based his wireless power transmission on the observation that:
1. **Crookes Tube Phenomenon**: Low-pressure gases become highly conductive
2. **Atmospheric Layers**: Upper atmosphere at 30,000+ feet has low pressure and high conductivity
3. **Global Circuit**: Earth and ionosphere form a natural electrical circuit
4. **Resonance**: Proper frequency tuning could enable efficient power transmission

### Technical Implementation
Tesla's patents describe:
1. **Transmitting Stations**: High-voltage, high-frequency generators connected to elevated terminals
2. **Receiving Stations**: Tuned circuits to capture transmitted energy
3. **Earth Connection**: Using Earth as return conductor
4. **Frequency Tuning**: Specific frequencies for optimal transmission efficiency

This patent progression shows Tesla's systematic development from conventional AC power systems toward revolutionary wireless power transmission technology, providing the technical foundation for claims about "free energy" distribution.
