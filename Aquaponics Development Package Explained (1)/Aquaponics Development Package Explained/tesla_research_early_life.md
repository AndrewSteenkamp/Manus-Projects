# Tesla Research: Early Life and Background

## Basic Information
- **Full Name**: Nikola Tesla (Никола Тесла)
- **Born**: July 10, 1856, in Smiljan, Austrian Empire (now Croatia)
- **Died**: January 7, 1943 (aged 86), New York City, U.S.
- **Nationality**: Serbian-American (naturalized U.S. citizen from 1891)
- **Occupation**: Engineer, futurist, inventor

## Family Background
- **Father**: Milutin Tesla (1819-1879), Eastern Orthodox priest
- **Mother**: Georgina "Đuka" Mandić (1822-1892), daughter of an Orthodox priest
- **Family**: Fourth of five children in ethnic Serbian family
- **Mother's Influence**: Tesla credited his eidetic memory and creative abilities to his mother's genetics and influence. She had talent for making home craft tools and mechanical appliances and could memorize Serbian epic poems, despite having no formal education.

## Early Education and Development
### Primary Education (1861-1862)
- Attended primary school in Smiljan studying German, arithmetic, and religion
- Family moved to nearby Gospić where father worked as parish priest
- Completed primary and middle school

### High School (1870-1873)
- Moved to Karlovac to attend Higher Real Gymnasium
- Classes held in German (standard in Austro-Hungarian Military Frontier)
- Became interested in physics through professor's electricity demonstrations
- Could perform integral calculus in his head (teachers suspected cheating)
- Finished four-year term in three years, graduating in 1873

### Health Crisis and Recovery
- Contracted cholera after graduation, bedridden for nine months, near death
- Father promised to send him to best engineering school if he recovered
- Read Mark Twain's works during recovery

### Military Service Evasion and College
- Evaded conscription into Austro-Hungarian Army by fleeing to mountains
- Enrolled at Imperial-Royal Technical College in Graz (1875) on Military Frontier scholarship
- Passed nine exams (nearly twice required), received commendation as "star of first rank"
- Fascinated by Professor Jakob Pöschl's electricity lectures
- Failed by third year, left Graz in December 1878 without graduating
- Possibly expelled for gambling and womanizing

## Early Career Path
### Missing Period (1878-1879)
- Family lost contact after leaving school
- Rumored drowned, but found working in Maribor
- Worked as draftsman for 60 florins per month
- Spent free time playing cards with local men

### Immigration to America (1884)
- Gained practical experience in telephony and at Continental Edison
- Immigrated to United States in 1884
- Became naturalized citizen
- Worked briefly at Edison Machine Works in New York City before striking out on own

## Key Early Characteristics
- Demonstrated exceptional mathematical abilities from young age
- Strong interest in electricity and "mysterious phenomena"
- Showed early signs of inventive genius
- Had eidetic memory inherited from mother
- Physical and mental strength developed through contact with nature



## Career Progression

### Budapest Telephone Exchange (1881)
- Worked under Tivadar Puskás at telegraph company
- Became chief electrician when company became functional
- Made improvements to Central Station equipment including telephone repeater/amplifier

### Continental Edison Company, Paris (1882)
- Worked in brand new electric power industry
- Installed indoor incandescent lighting citywide in large scale electric power utility
- Worked at Société Électrique Edison in Ivry-sur-Seine suburb
- Gained practical experience in electrical engineering
- Management noticed his advanced knowledge in engineering and physics
- Soon had him designing and building improved versions of generating dynamos and motors

### Immigration to America (1884)
- Edison manager Charles Batchelor brought Tesla to United States
- Worked at Edison Machine Works on Manhattan's Lower East Side
- Overcrowded shop with hundreds of machinists, laborers, managing staff and 20 "field engineers"
- Tesla worked on troubleshooting installations and improving generators

### The Edison Dispute
- Tesla may have met Thomas Edison only a couple of times
- One notable incident: after staying up all night repairing damaged dynamos on SS Oregon, Tesla ran into Batchelor and Edison
- Edison commented "this is a damned good man"
- **The $50,000 Bonus Controversy**: Tesla was allegedly offered $50,000 bonus to design "twenty-four different types of standard machines" but Edison later reneged, saying "Tesla, you don't understand our American humor"
- Tesla had been working at Machine Works for six months when he quit
- Tesla's diary entry (Dec 7, 1884 - Jan 4, 1885): "Good By to the Edison Machine Works"

### Tesla Electric Light and Manufacturing Company (1885)
- Soon after leaving Edison, Tesla worked on patenting arc lighting system
- Met with patent attorney Lemuel W. Serrell (same attorney used by Edison)
- Introduced Tesla to two businessmen, Robert Lane and Benjamin Vail
- They agreed to finance arc lighting manufacturing and utility company
- **Tesla Electric Light and Manufacturing Company** formed
- Tesla obtained patents including improved DC generator - first patents issued to Tesla in US
- Built and installed system in Rahway, New Jersey

### Early Setback
- Investors showed little interest in Tesla's ideas for new types of alternating current motors and electrical transmission equipment
