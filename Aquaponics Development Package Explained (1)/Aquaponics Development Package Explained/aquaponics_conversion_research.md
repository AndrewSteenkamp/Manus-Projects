# Aquaponics Conversion Research Notes

## Three Methods to Convert Hydroponics to Aquaponics

### Method 1: Entire System Switch-over
- Drain system and replace all water
- Plant seedlings and hang towers
- Cycle for 6 weeks
- Add fish
- **Problem:** Significant production decrease during 6-week cycling
- **Best for:** Hobby growers or those not dependent on continuous production

### Method 2: Temporary Dual Systems (RECOMMENDED FOR COMMERCIAL)
- Build aquaponic system alongside existing hydroponics
- Cycle new aquaponic system for 4-6 weeks independently
- Transfer hydroponic towers to cycled aquaponic system once ready
- Deconstruct old hydroponic system
- **Benefit:** NO decrease in production
- **Cost:** Some unused hydroponic equipment afterward
- **Note:** Nutrient solution in towers won't harm fish (only tablespoons transfer)

### Method 3: Permanent Dual Systems
- Build aquaponic system alongside hydroponics
- Cycle for 4-6 weeks
- Move most towers to aquaponics
- Keep hydroponics as "treatment system" for deficiencies
- **Benefit:** Can treat nutrient deficiencies quickly
- **Benefit:** Backup system if catastrophe strikes
- **Cost:** Need space for both systems

## Key Technical Points

### System Cycling
- 6-week cycling period required for new aquaponic systems
- Can reduce to 4 weeks if using inoculated towers
- Biological Surface Area (BSA) important for good cycling
- Nitrification must be established before adding fish

### Fish Compatibility
- Hydroponic fertilizers and fish don't mix
- Fish sensitive to toxins and chemical compounds
- Must research nutrients and pesticides carefully
- Can't use same chemicals as hydroponics

### Feed Ratios
- 60-100 grams of fish feed per square meter of plant growing area per day
- 0.3 pounds of fish per square foot of raft area recommended

## Important Warnings
- Don't switch hastily without thorough research
- Hydroponics has more versatility than aquaponics
- Fish are sensitive - must be careful with all inputs
- Research all nutrients and pesticides before use
