# AI-Powered UGC Ads Agency Platform - User Guide

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the AI-Powered UGC Ads Agency Platform! This platform is designed to automate the entire process of creating and managing UGC-style video ads for e-commerce brands across all major platforms. This user guide will walk you through the key features and functionality of the platform.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1. Accessing the Platform

To access the platform, navigate to the frontend dashboard URL provided during deployment. You will be prompted to log in with your credentials.

### 2.2. Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is divided into four main sections:

*   **Overview:** Provides a high-level summary of your clients, projects, and ads.
*   **Clients:** Allows you to manage your clients.
*   **Projects:** Allows you to manage your projects.
*   **Ads:** Allows you to view and manage your ads.

## 3. Managing Clients

### 3.1. Adding a New Client

To add a new client:

1.  Click on the "Clients" tab in the navigation bar.
2.  Click the "Add Client" button in the top-right corner.
3.  Fill in the client details:
    *   **Name:** The name of the client.
    *   **Email:** The client's email address.
    *   **Shopify Store URL:** The URL of the client's e-commerce store.
4.  Click "Create Client" to add the client to the system.

### 3.2. Viewing Client Details

To view a client's details:

1.  Click on the "Clients" tab in the navigation bar.
2.  Click on the client's name in the list.

### 3.3. Editing Client Details

To edit a client's details:

1.  Click on the "Clients" tab in the navigation bar.
2.  Click on the client's name in the list.
3.  Click the "Edit" button.
4.  Update the client details.
5.  Click "Save Changes" to update the client information.

## 4. Managing Projects

### 4.1. Creating a New Project

To create a new project:

1.  Click on the "Projects" tab in the navigation bar.
2.  Click the "Add Project" button in the top-right corner.
3.  Select a client from the dropdown menu.
4.  Fill in the project details:
    *   **Product Name:** The name of the product.
    *   **Product Category:** The category of the product (e.g., supplements, electronics, beauty).
    *   **Product URL:** The URL of the product page.
    *   **Product Description:** A brief description of the product.
5.  Click "Create Project" to create the project.

### 4.2. Viewing Project Details

To view a project's details:

1.  Click on the "Projects" tab in the navigation bar.
2.  Click on the project's name in the list.

### 4.3. Starting Ad Generation

To start generating ads for a project:

1.  Click on the "Projects" tab in the navigation bar.
2.  Click on the project's name in the list.
3.  Click the "Generate Ads" button.
4.  The system will automatically:
    *   Research pain points for the product category.
    *   Generate ad scripts based on the pain points.
    *   Create UGC-style videos using AI avatars.
    *   Save the generated ads to the system.

## 5. Managing Ads

### 5.1. Viewing Ads

To view all ads:

1.  Click on the "Ads" tab in the navigation bar.
2.  Use the filters to narrow down the list by client, project, or status.

### 5.2. Reviewing and Approving Ads

To review and approve ads:

1.  Click on the "Ads" tab in the navigation bar.
2.  Click on an ad to view its details.
3.  Watch the video and review the script.
4.  Click "Approve" to mark the ad as approved, or "Reject" to request changes.

### 5.3. Downloading Ads

To download an ad:

1.  Click on the "Ads" tab in the navigation bar.
2.  Click on an ad to view its details.
3.  Click the "Download" button to download the video file.

## 6. Lead Generation

### 6.1. Finding New Leads

The platform includes a lead generation system that can automatically find potential clients across all major e-commerce platforms. To use this feature:

1.  Navigate to the "Lead Generation" section.
2.  Select the e-commerce platforms you want to target (e.g., Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento).
3.  Select the product categories you want to target.
4.  Click "Generate Leads" to start the lead generation process.
5.  The system will use BuiltWith and Apollo.io to find potential clients and verify their contact information.

### 6.2. Managing Leads

To manage your leads:

1.  Navigate to the "Leads" section.
2.  Use the filters to narrow down the list by platform, category, or status.
3.  Click on a lead to view its details.
4.  Update the lead status as needed.

## 7. Cold Outreach

### 7.1. Creating Email Campaigns

The platform includes a cold outreach system that can automatically send personalized emails to potential clients. To create an email campaign:

1.  Navigate to the "Outreach" section.
2.  Click "Create Email Campaign".
3.  Select the leads you want to target.
4.  Choose an email template or create a new one.
5.  Schedule the campaign.
6.  Click "Start Campaign" to begin sending emails.

### 7.2. Creating Social Media Campaigns

The platform can also automate social media outreach. To create a social media campaign:

1.  Navigate to the "Outreach" section.
2.  Click "Create Social Campaign".
3.  Select the platform (e.g., LinkedIn, Twitter).
4.  Select the leads you want to target.
5.  Choose a message template or create a new one.
6.  Schedule the campaign.
7.  Click "Start Campaign" to begin sending messages.

### 7.3. Monitoring Campaign Performance

To monitor the performance of your outreach campaigns:

1.  Navigate to the "Outreach" section.
2.  Click on the "Analytics" tab.
3.  View metrics such as open rates, click rates, and response rates.
4.  Use the filters to narrow down the data by campaign, platform, or date range.

## 8. Best Practices

### 8.1. Ad Creation

*   **Product Research:** Provide as much detail as possible about the product to help the AI generate more accurate pain points and scripts.
*   **Ad Approval:** Review all generated ads before sending them to clients to ensure quality and accuracy.
*   **Iteration:** If an ad doesn't meet your standards, reject it and generate a new one with more specific instructions.

### 8.2. Lead Generation

*   **Targeting:** Be specific about the platforms and categories you want to target to get more qualified leads.
*   **Verification:** Always verify email addresses before sending cold emails to avoid bounces and protect your sender reputation.
*   **Segmentation:** Segment your leads by platform, category, and company size to create more personalized outreach campaigns.

### 8.3. Cold Outreach

*   **Personalization:** Use the platform's personalization features to make your emails and messages feel more personal and relevant.
*   **Follow-up:** Set up automated follow-up sequences for leads that don't respond to your initial outreach.
*   **A/B Testing:** Test different subject lines, templates, and sending times to optimize your outreach campaigns.

## 9. Troubleshooting

### 9.1. Common Issues

*   **Ad Generation Failures:** If ad generation fails, check that the product information is complete and try again.
*   **Email Delivery Issues:** If emails aren't being delivered, check that the email addresses are valid and that your sending domain has proper SPF and DKIM records.
*   **API Rate Limits:** If you're hitting rate limits with the AI tools or outreach services, try spreading your campaigns out over a longer period.

### 9.2. Getting Help

If you encounter any issues that you can't resolve, please contact our support team at support@ugcadsagency.com.
