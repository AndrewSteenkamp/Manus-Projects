### How to Search on 1688.com:

**Option 1: Use ZQ's Search Tool**

**Option 2: Direct 1688.com Search**
1. Visit: **https://www.1688.com/**
2. Use Google Translate extension to translate the page

4. Look for suppliers with:

### What to Look For:
- **Price range**: $2-15 per unit (your estimated costs)
- **Quality**: Check product photos and reviews
- **MOQ**: Ideally 1-10 pieces (start small)
- **Shipping**: Confirm supplier ships to ZQ's warehouse

## Step 3: Request Quotes from ZQ

Once you find products on 1688.com:

1. **Copy the 1688.com product URL**
2. **Submit to ZQ Dropshipping**:
   - Go to ZQ dashboard → "Product Sourcing"
   - Paste the 1688.com URL
   - Specify quantity (start with 10-20 units per product)
   - Request quote

3. **ZQ will provide**:
   - Product cost (from 1688.com supplier)
   - ZQ service fee (usually 5-10% of product cost)
   - International shipping cost (to your customers)
   - Total landed cost per unit

4. **Compare with your pricing**:
   - Ensure your selling price covers:
     - Product cost
     - ZQ fees
     - Shipping
     - Your profit margin (target: 60-70%)

## Step 6: Set Up Automated Fulfillment

Once you're satisfied with product quality:

### Connect ZQ to Your Store:

**Option 1: Manual Order Processing** (Recommended for beginners)
1. When customer places order on Alpapies.com
2. You receive order notification (email)
3. Log into ZQ dashboard
4. Create order manually:
   - Enter customer shipping address
   - Select product from your ZQ inventory
   - Pay for product + shipping
5. ZQ ships directly to customer
6. ZQ provides tracking number
7. Update customer with tracking info

**Option 2: API Integration** (For automation later)
- ZQ offers API integration
- Automatically sync orders from Alpapies to ZQ
- Requires developer setup (can implement later)

### Payment to ZQ:
- ZQ accepts: PayPal, credit card, bank transfer
- You pay ZQ after customer pays you
- Typical payment flow:
  1. Customer pays you $24.99 (via PayFast)
  2. You pay ZQ $9 (product) + $5 (shipping) = $14
  3. Your profit: $10.99

## Step 7: Pricing Strategy

Your current pricing is based on estimates. After getting real quotes from ZQ, adjust if needed:

### Pricing Formula:
```
Selling Price = (Product Cost + ZQ Fee + Shipping + VAT) × 2.5
```

### Example: iPhone 16 MagSafe Case
- **1688.com cost**: $6
- **ZQ service fee**: $0.60 (10%)

### Adjust Prices If Needed:

## Step 8: Shipping Times & Customer Expectations

### Typical Shipping Timeline:
- **Processing**: 1-3 days (ZQ sources and ships)
- **Shipping**: 10-20 days (China to South Africa via ePacket/4PX)
- **Total**: 11-23 days

### Set Customer Expectations:
1. Update website shipping policy:
   - "Delivery: 10-20 business days"
   - "Free worldwide shipping on orders over $50"
   - "Tracking provided for all orders"

2. Add to product pages:
   - "Ships from: China (via ZQ Dropshipping)"
   - "Estimated delivery: 2-3 weeks"

### Faster Shipping Options (Premium):
- **DHL/FedEx**: 5-7 days (costs $15-25 per order)
- Offer as "Express Shipping" upgrade at checkout
- Charge customer $10-15 extra

## Step 9: Quality Control

### ZQ's Quality Inspection:
- ZQ inspects products before shipping
- Checks for defects, damage, incorrect items
- Takes photos if requested ($1-2 per order)

### Your Quality Standards:
- Reject products with:
  - Visible defects
  - Wrong color/model
  - Poor packaging
  - Damaged items

### Handling Defects:
1. Customer reports issue
2. Request photos from customer
3. Contact ZQ support
4. ZQ investigates with supplier
5. Options:
   - Full refund
   - Replacement shipment
   - Partial refund

## Common Questions

**Q: How much capital do I need to start?**
A: $100-300 for samples + initial inventory. You can fulfill orders on-demand (no inventory) but shipping takes longer.

**Q: What if customers complain about shipping time?**
A: Set clear expectations on website. Offer express shipping upgrade. Consider ZQ's US/EU warehouses later for faster delivery.

**Q: How do I handle returns?**
A: Create a return policy (14-30 days). For defective products, ZQ covers replacement. For buyer's remorse, customer pays return shipping to China (expensive, so offer partial refund instead).

**Q: Can I brand the products?**
A: Yes! Many 1688.com suppliers offer custom packaging and branding. Minimum order: 100-500 units. Cost: $0.50-2 per unit extra.

**Q: What if ZQ is out of stock?**
A: Have backup suppliers. Search 1688.com for alternative products. Communicate delays to customers immediately.

**Q: How do I track orders?**
A: ZQ provides tracking numbers for all shipments. Integrate tracking into your website's order status page.
