## Understanding Your Dashboard

When you open the Admin Dashboard, you'll see:

### 📊 Statistics Cards (Top of Page)
1. **Total Products**: How many products are in your store (currently 9 sample products)
2. **Total Orders**: How many orders customers have placed
3. **Total Revenue**: How much money you've made
4. **Avg Order Value**: Average amount per order

### 📦 Recent Orders
- Shows the latest customer orders
- Click "View All" to see all orders
- Each order shows:
  - Order number
  - Customer name
  - Amount
  - Status (pending, completed, etc.)

### ⚡ Quick Actions
Two main buttons:

1. **Add New Product** - Click this to add a new product to your store
2. **Manage Suppliers** - Click this to view/edit your supplier information (ZQ Dropshipping)
