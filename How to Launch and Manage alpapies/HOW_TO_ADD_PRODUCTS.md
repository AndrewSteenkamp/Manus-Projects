# How to Add Products to Your Store
**A Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners**

This guide shows you exactly how to add products to your Alpapies store. No coding required!

---

## Before You Start

### What You'll Need:
1. **Product Information** from your supplier (ZQ Dropshipping):
   - Product name
   - Description
   - Price (what you pay)
   - Photos
   - SKU (product code)
   
2. **Your Selling Price**:
   - Calculate: Supplier Price + Your Profit + Shipping
   - Example: If supplier charges $10, you might sell for $24.99

3. **Product Photos**:
   - Get high-quality images from your supplier
   - Or download from 1688.com product pages

---

## Method 1: Using the Admin Dashboard (Easiest)

### Step 1: Open Your Admin Dashboard
1. Go to: https://3000-ipdoqrys3o2pwm5io77gj-041a252d.us2.manus.computer/admin
2. You should see your dashboard with statistics

### Step 2: Click "Add New Product"
1. Look for the blue card that says "Add New Product"
2. Click on it
3. A form will open

### Step 3: Fill in Product Information

**Basic Information:**
- **Product Name**: Give it a clear, descriptive name
  - Good: "Premium Leather Wallet Case - iPhone 15 Pro"
  - Bad: "Case 1"
  
- **Description**: Explain what makes it special
  - Include features and benefits
  - Example: "Genuine leather wallet case with card slots and RFID protection. Premium quality with kickstand."

- **SKU**: Your product code
  - Example: "LC-IP15P-BLK"
  - Make it unique for each product

**Pricing:**
- **Original Price**: The "before" price (for showing discount)
  - Example: $39.99
  
- **Sale Price**: Your actual selling price
  - Example: $24.99
  
- **Cost Price**: What you pay the supplier (for your records)
  - Example: $10.00

**Category:**
- Select from dropdown:
  - Phone Cases
  - Screen Protectors
  - Chargers & Cables
  - Audio Accessories
  - Wireless Charging
  - (More categories can be added)

**Stock:**
- **In Stock**: Yes/No
- **Stock Quantity**: How many you have (or leave blank for dropshipping)

**Images:**
- Click "Upload Image"
- Select product photo from your computer
- Add multiple angles if available

**Additional Options:**
- **Featured Product**: Check this box to show on homepage
- **Supplier**: Select "ZQ Dropshipping" from dropdown
- **Supplier Product URL**: Paste the 1688.com link

### Step 4: Save the Product
1. Review all information
2. Click "Save Product" button
3. You'll see a success message
4. Product now appears in your store!

## Tips for Great Product Listings

### Product Names:
✅ **Good**: "65W GaN Fast Charger - USB-C PD"
- Clear, specific, includes key features

❌ **Bad**: "Charger"
- Too vague, customers won't understand

### Descriptions:
✅ **Good**: 
"65W GaN fast charger with USB-C Power Delivery. Compact design 40% smaller than traditional chargers. Universal compatibility with iPhone, Samsung, laptops. Includes 6ft braided cable."

❌ **Bad**:
"Good charger. Fast."

### Pricing Strategy:
1. **Check competitors**: Look at Amazon, eBay prices
2. **Calculate costs**:
   - Supplier cost: $10
   - Shipping: $5
   - Your profit: $10
   - Total: $25
3. **Set original price higher**: $39.99
4. **Set sale price**: $24.99
5. **Customers see 38% discount!**

### Photos:
- Use supplier's photos (they're professional)
- Show product from multiple angles
- Include size comparison if possible
- Make sure images are clear and high-quality

Let's walk through adding a real product:

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Mistake 1**: Forgetting to add product images
- Customers won't buy without photos!

❌ **Mistake 2**: Pricing too low
- Don't forget shipping costs and your profit

❌ **Mistake 3**: Vague descriptions
- Tell customers exactly what they're getting

❌ **Mistake 4**: Wrong category
- Customers can't find your product if it's miscategorized

❌ **Mistake 5**: Not checking the live site
- Always preview how it looks to customers

1. Go to 1688.com
