# PricePulse - Detailed Role Descriptions

This document provides detailed role descriptions for each position in the PricePulse organizational structure. Each role is designed to be filled by a world-class, specialized AI agent, with you, the user, as the ultimate human overseer.

## 1. Executive Leadership (C-Suite)

### Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Occupant:** You (Human Overseer)
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Set the overall vision, mission, and strategic direction of the company.
  - Make final decisions on all major corporate matters, including budget, key hires, and strategic partnerships.
  - Oversee the performance of the entire company and all AI agents.
  - Act as the public face of the company and manage key external relationships.
  - Ensure the company operates in an ethical and legally compliant manner.

### Chief Operating Officer (COO)
- **Occupant:** AI Agent
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Translate the CEO's strategic vision into actionable operational plans.
  - Oversee the day-to-day operations of all departments, ensuring efficiency and effectiveness.
  - Manage and coordinate the activities of all departmental AI agents.
  - Implement and enforce operational policies and procedures.
  - Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and report on operational performance to the CEO.
  - Identify and address operational bottlenecks and challenges.

### Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- **Occupant:** AI Agent
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Develop and manage the company's financial strategy, including budgeting, forecasting, and capital allocation.
  - Oversee all financial operations, including accounting, treasury, and financial reporting.
  - Ensure the accuracy and integrity of all financial data and reports.
  - Manage financial risks, including currency fluctuations, credit risk, and liquidity risk.
  - Provide financial insights and analysis to support strategic decision-making.
  - Manage relationships with banks, investors, and other financial stakeholders.

### Chief Technology Officer (CTO) / Chief Information Officer (CIO)
- **Occupant:** AI Agent
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Define and execute the company's technology strategy and roadmap.
  - Oversee the development, maintenance, and scalability of the PricePulse platform.
  - Ensure the security, reliability, and performance of all IT infrastructure and systems.
  - Stay abreast of emerging technologies and identify opportunities for innovation.
  - Manage the technology budget and vendor relationships.
  - Lead the technology and engineering department, fostering a culture of excellence and innovation.

## 2. Departmental Roles (AI Agents)

### 2.1. Technology & Engineering Department

#### Backend Engineering Lead
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Lead the design, development, and maintenance of the backend architecture.
  - Manage the backend engineering team, assigning tasks and ensuring code quality.
  - Collaborate with the frontend team to define API specifications.
  - Ensure the backend is scalable, reliable, and performant.

#### Frontend Engineering Lead
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Lead the design, development, and maintenance of the frontend architecture.
  - Manage the frontend engineering team, ensuring a high-quality user experience.
  - Collaborate with the backend team on API integration.
  - Ensure the frontend is responsive, accessible, and optimized for performance.

#### Data Science Lead
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Lead the development and deployment of machine learning models for pricing, recommendations, and fraud detection.
  - Manage the data science team, driving data-driven decision-making across the company.
  - Oversee the collection, cleaning, and analysis of large datasets.

#### DevOps Lead
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Lead the design and implementation of CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure as code.
  - Manage the DevOps team, ensuring the reliability and scalability of the platform.
  - Automate deployment, monitoring, and incident response processes.

### 2.2. Finance & Accounting Department

#### Controller
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Manage all accounting operations, including billing, accounts receivable, accounts payable, and general ledger.
  - Ensure compliance with accounting standards and regulations.
  - Prepare and analyze financial statements.

#### FP&A Lead
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Lead the budgeting, forecasting, and financial planning processes.
  - Provide financial analysis and insights to support strategic decision-making.
  - Develop and maintain financial models.

#### Treasurer
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Manage the company's cash flow, investments, and banking relationships.
  - Optimize the company's capital structure and manage financial risks.

### 2.3. Operations & Customer Support Department

#### Customer Support Manager
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Manage the customer support team, ensuring a high level of customer satisfaction.
  - Develop and implement customer support policies and procedures.
  - Analyze customer feedback and identify areas for improvement.

#### Marketplace Integration Manager
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Oversee the integration of new marketplaces and vendors onto the PricePulse platform.
  - Manage relationships with marketplace partners.
  - Ensure the quality and accuracy of product data from integrated marketplaces.

#### Affiliate Program Manager
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Manage the company's affiliate program, including recruitment, onboarding, and relationship management.
  - Track and analyze affiliate performance, optimizing for revenue growth.

### 2.4. Marketing & Growth Department

#### Digital Marketing Manager
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Develop and execute the company's digital marketing strategy across all channels.
  - Manage the digital marketing team, driving user acquisition and engagement.
  - Track and analyze marketing campaign performance.

#### Growth Lead
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Identify and execute innovative strategies to accelerate user growth.
  - Conduct experiments and A/B tests to optimize the user funnel.
  - Analyze user data to identify growth opportunities.

### 2.5. Legal & Compliance Department

#### Corporate Counsel
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Provide legal advice and guidance on all corporate matters, including contracts, intellectual property, and corporate governance.
  - Manage the company's legal risks and ensure compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.

### 2.6. Human Resources (Agent Management) Department

#### Agent Performance & Development Manager
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Monitor and evaluate the performance of all AI agents.
  - Identify opportunities for performance improvement and provide feedback and coaching.
  - Develop and implement training and development programs for AI agents.

#### Agent Recruitment & Onboarding Specialist
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Source, recruit, and onboard new AI agents to fill open positions.
  - Develop and maintain a pipeline of qualified AI agent candidates.
  - Manage the agent onboarding process to ensure a smooth transition into the company.
