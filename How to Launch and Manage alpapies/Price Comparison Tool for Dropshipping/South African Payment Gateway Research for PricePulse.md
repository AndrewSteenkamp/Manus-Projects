# South African Payment Gateway Research for PricePulse

## Overview

This research evaluates the best payment gateways in South Africa that support international payments, which is crucial for PricePulse to receive affiliate commissions from global marketplaces.

## Top Payment Gateway Options

### 1. Payfast
- **Website:** https://payfast.io/
- **International Payments:** Yes, supports Visa and Mastercard credit cards from around the world.
- **Fees:** 
  - Credit & Cheque Cards: 3.5% + R2.00
  - International Cards: 5% + R2.00
- **Features:**
  - Wide range of payment methods (credit cards, Instant EFT, QR codes, etc.)
  - Integrates with over 70 shopping carts and platforms.
  - Recurring billing and tokenization.
  - No monthly or setup fees.

### 2. Peach Payments
- **Website:** https://www.peachpayments.com/
- **International Payments:** Yes, supports international Mastercard, Visa, and Amex cards.
- **Fees:**
  - Growth Plan: 2.95% + R1.50 per transaction.
  - Enterprise Plan: Custom volume-based rates.
  - International Cards: 3.50% + R1.50 per transaction.
- **Features:**
  - Multi-currency selling.
  - Integrates with major e-commerce platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.).
  - Payment links, payouts, and payment orchestration.
  - 365-day support.

### 3. Yoco
- **Website:** https://www.yoco.com/
- **International Payments:** Yes, accepts all major international cards.
- **Fees:**
  - Online Payments: 2.95% (ex. VAT) per transaction.
- **Features:**
  - Online payment gateway and card machines.
  - Simple integration with websites and e-commerce platforms.
  - Yoco Link for accepting payments via link.

### 4. Ozow
- **Website:** https://ozow.com/
- **International Payments:** Primarily focused on EFT payments within South Africa, but supports international cards through partnerships.
- **Fees:** 
  - From 2.5% per transaction.
- **Features:**
  - Instant EFT payments.
  - Pay by Bank and Bank API solutions.
  - QR code payments.

### 5. Netcash
- **Website:** https://netcash.co.za/
- **International Payments:** Yes, supports international credit cards.
- **Fees:**
  - Custom pricing based on volume and business needs.
- **Features:**
  - Comprehensive suite of payment solutions (gateway, debit orders, salary payments, etc.).
  - Integrates with accounting software.

## Recommendation for PricePulse

**Primary Choice: Peach Payments**
- **Reasoning:** Peach Payments offers a comprehensive solution for international payments with multi-currency support, which is ideal for PricePulse's global business model. Their fees are competitive, and they provide excellent support and integration options.

**Secondary Choice: Payfast**
- **Reasoning:** Payfast is a well-established and reliable payment gateway in South Africa with a wide range of payment methods. Their international fees are slightly higher than Peach Payments, but they are a solid alternative.

## Implementation Strategy

1.  **Sign up for Peach Payments:** Create an account with Peach Payments and complete the verification process.
2.  **Integrate with PricePulse Backend:** Use the Peach Payments API to integrate the payment gateway into the PricePulse backend.
3.  **Implement Payouts:** Configure the payouts feature to transfer funds from your Peach Payments account to your FNB business account.
4.  **Test Transactions:** Conduct test transactions to ensure that the payment gateway is working correctly.
5.  **Monitor and Optimize:** Monitor transaction fees and success rates, and optimize the payment process as needed.
