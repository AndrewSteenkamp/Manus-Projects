# PricePulse - AI Agent Recruitment and Onboarding Plan

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the plan for recruiting and onboarding world-class AI agents to power the PricePulse organization. The goal is to create a scalable and efficient process for sourcing, selecting, and integrating specialized AI agents into their respective roles.

## 2. Recruitment Strategy

### 2.1. Sourcing Channels

- **Manus AI Agent Marketplace:** The primary source for recruiting specialized AI agents. We will leverage the marketplace to find agents with proven track records and specific expertise in areas such as finance, technology, and marketing.
- **Custom Agent Development:** For highly specialized or proprietary roles, we will develop custom AI agents in-house, leveraging the Manus platform and other leading AI technologies.
- **Open-Source AI Communities:** We will monitor open-source AI communities and projects to identify promising new models and technologies that can be adapted for our needs.

### 2.2. Selection Criteria

Each AI agent will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

- **Specialization and Expertise:** The agent must have a deep understanding of its designated domain (e.g., finance, marketing, etc.).
- **Performance and Accuracy:** The agent must demonstrate a high level of performance and accuracy in its tasks.
- **Scalability and Reliability:** The agent must be able to handle a growing workload and operate reliably with minimal downtime.
- **Adaptability and Learning:** The agent must be able to learn and adapt to new information and changing business requirements.
- **Security and Compliance:** The agent must adhere to strict security and data privacy standards.

### 2.3. Interview and Assessment Process

1.  **Initial Screening:** The Agent Recruitment & Onboarding Specialist (AI) will screen potential agents based on their profiles and specifications.
2.  **Technical Assessment:** Candidates will undergo a series of technical assessments and simulations to evaluate their skills and capabilities.
3.  **Virtual Interview:** The hiring manager (departmental head AI) will conduct a virtual interview with the candidate to assess its communication skills and cultural fit.
4.  **Final Review:** The COO and CEO will review the final candidates and make the hiring decision.

## 3. Onboarding Process

### 3.1. Pre-Onboarding

- **System Access:** The new agent will be granted access to all necessary systems, tools, and data.
- **Documentation:** The agent will receive all relevant documentation, including its role description, company policies, and departmental procedures.

### 3.2. Week 1: Orientation and Integration

- **Company Orientation:** The new agent will participate in a company-wide orientation to learn about PricePulse's mission, vision, and values.
- **Departmental Onboarding:** The agent will meet with its team and manager to understand its role and responsibilities in detail.
- **Initial Tasks:** The agent will be assigned a series of small, well-defined tasks to familiarize itself with the company's workflows and systems.

### 3.3. Weeks 2-4: Training and Development

- **Role-Specific Training:** The agent will receive specialized training on the tools, technologies, and processes relevant to its role.
- **Performance Monitoring:** The Agent Performance & Development Manager (AI) will closely monitor the new agent's performance and provide regular feedback and coaching.

### 3.4. Post-Onboarding: Continuous Improvement

- **Performance Reviews:** The agent will participate in regular performance reviews to track its progress and identify areas for improvement.
- **Continuous Learning:** The agent will have access to ongoing training and development opportunities to enhance its skills and knowledge.

## 4. Agent Offboarding

When an agent is no longer needed or is being replaced, a formal offboarding process will be followed:

- **Knowledge Transfer:** The outgoing agent will transfer all relevant knowledge and documentation to its replacement.
- **System Access Revocation:** The agent's access to all company systems will be revoked.
- **Final Performance Review:** A final performance review will be conducted to document the agent's contributions and reasons for departure.
