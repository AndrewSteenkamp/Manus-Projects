## 1688.com and ZQ Dropshipping Analysis

### 1688.com Platform Overview
**Key Characteristics:**
- China's largest domestic wholesale platform (equivalent to Alibaba.com for domestic market)
- Offers lowest wholesale prices compared to AliExpress
- Primarily targets domestic Chinese sales
- Does not ship internationally directly
- Only accepts China Alipay and Chinese debit cards
- Language barrier: Only available in Chinese
- Requires intermediary services for international dropshipping

**Advantages:**
- Lowest wholesale pricing available
- Direct access to manufacturers
- Vast product selection
- Same suppliers that serve Temu and Shein

**Challenges:**
- No international shipping
- Payment method limitations
- Language barrier
- Quality control concerns (as mentioned by user)
- Difficulty in supplier verification
