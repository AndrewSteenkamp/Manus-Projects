## Agent Hierarchy & Authority Levels

### Level 1: Autonomous Operations (No MD Approval Required)
- **Budget Authority**: Up to $1,000/day per agent
- **Decision Scope**: Routine operations, standard processes
- **Agents**: Customer Service, Order Processing, Content Creation

### Level 2: Supervised Autonomy (MD Notification)
- **Budget Authority**: Up to $5,000/day per agent  
- **Decision Scope**: Inventory management, marketing campaigns
- **Agents**: Inventory Optimizer, Marketing Automation, Supplier Manager

### Level 3: Strategic Decisions (MD Approval Required)
- **Budget Authority**: Over $5,000 or strategic impact
- **Decision Scope**: New product lines, major partnerships, expansion
- **Agents**: Strategic Planner, Executive Dashboard, Risk Assessor
