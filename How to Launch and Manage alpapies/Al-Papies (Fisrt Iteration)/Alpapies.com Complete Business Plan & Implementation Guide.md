## Risk Analysis & Mitigation

### Business Risks

**1. Supplier Dependency Risk**
- **Risk**: Over-reliance on ZQ Dropshipping
- **Mitigation**: Develop relationships with 2-3 backup suppliers
- **Monitoring**: Regular supplier performance reviews

**2. Market Competition Risk**
- **Risk**: Increased competition from established players
- **Mitigation**: Focus on customer service and brand differentiation
- **Monitoring**: Regular competitive analysis

**3. Product Quality Risk**
- **Risk**: Poor quality products damage brand reputation
- **Mitigation**: Rigorous quality control with ZQ Dropshipping
- **Monitoring**: Customer feedback and return rate tracking

**4. Technology Risk**
- **Risk**: Website downtime or technical issues
- **Mitigation**: Reliable hosting, regular backups, monitoring
- **Monitoring**: Uptime monitoring and performance tracking

### Financial Risks

**1. Cash Flow Risk**
- **Risk**: Seasonal fluctuations in sales
- **Mitigation**: Diversify product categories, build cash reserves
- **Monitoring**: Monthly cash flow analysis

**2. Currency Risk**
- **Risk**: Exchange rate fluctuations with Chinese suppliers
- **Mitigation**: Price adjustments, hedging strategies
- **Monitoring**: Regular price review and adjustment

**3. Payment Processing Risk**
- **Risk**: Chargebacks and payment disputes
- **Mitigation**: Clear policies, excellent customer service
- **Monitoring**: Chargeback rate tracking

### Operational Risks

**1. Inventory Risk**
- **Risk**: Out-of-stock situations
- **Mitigation**: Real-time inventory sync, multiple suppliers
- **Monitoring**: Inventory level alerts

**2. Shipping Risk**
- **Risk**: Delayed or lost shipments
- **Mitigation**: Multiple shipping options, tracking systems
- **Monitoring**: Shipping performance metrics

**3. Customer Service Risk**
- **Risk**: Poor customer experience
- **Mitigation**: Comprehensive FAQ, responsive support
- **Monitoring**: Customer satisfaction surveys
