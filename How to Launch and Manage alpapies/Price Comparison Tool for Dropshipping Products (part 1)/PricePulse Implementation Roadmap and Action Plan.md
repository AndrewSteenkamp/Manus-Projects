


# PricePulse Implementation Roadmap and Action Plan

This document outlines the implementation roadmap and immediate action plan for the launch and scaling of PricePulse. It provides a step-by-step guide to deploying the platform, activating the AI agent workforce, and initiating revenue-generating activities.



## 1. Project Timeline

This timeline provides a high-level overview of the key milestones and deliverables for the launch and scaling of PricePulse.

| Phase | Milestone | Deliverables | Estimated Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-2)** | - Company Registration<br>- Bank Account Setup<br>- Platform Deployment | - Registered Company<br>- FNB Business Account<br>- Deployed PricePulse Platform | 2 Weeks |
| **Phase 2: Launch (Week 3)** | - AI Agent Activation<br>- Initial Marketing Campaign | - Activated AI Agents<br>- Live Marketing Campaigns | 1 Week |
| **Phase 3: Growth (Weeks 4-12)** | - User Acquisition<br>- Revenue Growth<br>- Platform Optimization | - 10,000+ Active Users<br>- $10,000+ Monthly Revenue<br>- Optimized Platform | 9 Weeks |
| **Phase 4: Expansion (Months 4-12)** | - International Expansion<br>- New Feature Development<br>- Team Expansion | - Presence in 10+ Countries<br>- 5+ New Features<br>- 50+ AI Agents | 9 Months |



## 2. Immediate Action Plan

This action plan outlines the immediate steps to be taken to launch PricePulse and begin generating revenue.

### Step 1: Company Registration and Bank Account Setup (Week 1)

*   **Action:** Follow the steps outlined in the "PricePulse SA Business Setup Guide" to register PricePulse as a private company in South Africa and open a business bank account with FNB.
*   **Responsibility:** You (the Director)
*   **Timeline:** 1 Week

### Step 2: Platform Deployment (Week 2)

*   **Action:** Deploy the PricePulse platform to a production environment.
*   **Responsibility:** CTO Agent
*   **Timeline:** 1 Week

### Step 3: AI Agent Activation (Week 3)

*   **Action:** Activate the AI agents for all departments.
*   **Responsibility:** COO Agent
*   **Timeline:** 1 Week

### Step 4: Initial Marketing Campaign (Week 3)

*   **Action:** Launch an initial marketing campaign to drive traffic to the PricePulse platform.
*   **Responsibility:** CMO Agent
*   **Timeline:** 1 Week

### Step 5: User Acquisition and Revenue Growth (Weeks 4-12)

*   **Action:** Execute on the marketing and sales strategy to acquire users and grow revenue.
*   **Responsibility:** CMO Agent, Sales Manager Agent
*   **Timeline:** 9 Weeks

### Step 6: Platform Optimization (Weeks 4-12)

*   **Action:** Continuously monitor and optimize the PricePulse platform for performance, scalability, and user experience.
*   **Responsibility:** CTO Agent
*   **Timeline:** 9 Weeks
