# PricePulse - Organizational Structure

## 1. Executive Leadership (C-Suite)

- **Chief Executive Officer (CEO):** You (Human Overseer)
  - *Role:* Provides strategic direction, vision, and final approval on all major decisions. Manages the overall business and ensures alignment with goals.
  - *Reports to:* N/A

- **Chief Operating Officer (COO):** AI Agent
  - *Role:* Oversees the day-to-day administrative and operational functions of the business. Manages all departmental AI agents and ensures operational efficiency.
  - *Reports to:* CEO

- **Chief Financial Officer (CFO):** AI Agent
  - *Role:* Responsible for all financial matters, including financial planning, risk management, and financial reporting. Manages the finance and accounting department.
  - *Reports to:* CEO

- **Chief Technology Officer (CTO) / Chief Information Officer (CIO):** AI Agent
  - *Role:* Manages the company's technology strategy, platform development, and IT infrastructure. Leads the technology and engineering department.
  - *Reports to:* CEO

## 2. Departments and Key Roles (AI Agents)

### 2.1. Technology & Engineering Department

- **Headed by:** CTO/CIO

- **Teams & Roles:**
  - **Platform Development Team:**
    - *Backend Engineering Lead (AI):* Manages the backend team and architecture.
    - *Backend Engineers (AI):* Develop and maintain the backend services, APIs, and databases.
    - *Frontend Engineering Lead (AI):* Manages the frontend team and architecture.
    - *Frontend Engineers (AI):* Develop and maintain the user interface and user experience.
  - **Data & AI Team:**
    - *Data Science Lead (AI):* Manages data analysis, machine learning models, and personalization.
    - *Data Engineers (AI):* Build and maintain data pipelines and infrastructure.
    - *AI/ML Engineers (AI):* Develop and deploy machine learning models for pricing, recommendations, and fraud detection.
  - **Infrastructure & DevOps Team:**
    - *DevOps Lead (AI):* Manages CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and site reliability.
    - *Site Reliability Engineers (AI):* Ensure platform uptime, performance, and scalability.

### 2.2. Finance & Accounting Department

- **Headed by:** CFO

- **Teams & Roles:**
  - **Accounting Team:**
    - *Controller (AI):* Manages accounting operations, including billing, A/R, A/P, and payroll.
    - *Accountants (AI):* Handle day-to-day bookkeeping and financial transactions.
  - **Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Team:**
    - *FP&A Lead (AI):* Manages budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis.
    - *Financial Analysts (AI):* Provide insights and analysis to support business decisions.
  - **Treasury & Payments Team:**
    - *Treasurer (AI):* Manages cash flow, investments, and banking relationships.
    - *Payment Specialists (AI):* Handle payment processing, international remittances, and currency management.
  - **Audit & Compliance Team:**
    - *Internal Auditor (AI):* Ensures compliance with financial regulations and internal policies.

### 2.3. Operations & Customer Support Department

- **Headed by:** COO

- **Teams & Roles:**
  - **Customer Support Team:**
    - *Customer Support Manager (AI):* Manages the support team and ensures customer satisfaction.
    - *Customer Support Agents (AI):* Provide multilingual support to users via email, chat, and other channels.
  - **Marketplace Operations Team:**
    - *Marketplace Integration Manager (AI):* Oversees the integration of new marketplaces and vendors.
    - *Data Quality Specialists (AI):* Ensure the accuracy and quality of product data and pricing information.
  - **Affiliate & Partner Operations Team:**
    - *Affiliate Program Manager (AI):* Manages relationships with affiliate partners and networks.

### 2.4. Marketing & Growth Department

- **Headed by:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) - AI Agent (Reports to COO)

- **Teams & Roles:**
  - **Digital Marketing Team:**
    - *Digital Marketing Manager (AI):* Manages all digital marketing channels.
    - *SEO/SEM Specialists (AI):* Drive organic and paid search traffic.
    - *Content Marketers (AI):* Create and distribute engaging content.
    - *Social Media Managers (AI):* Manage social media presence and community engagement.
  - **Growth Hacking Team:**
    - *Growth Lead (AI):* Identifies and executes strategies to accelerate user acquisition and growth.

### 2.5. Legal & Compliance Department

- **Headed by:** Chief Legal Officer (CLO) - AI Agent (Reports to COO)

- **Teams & Roles:**
  - **Corporate Counsel (AI):* Provides legal advice on all corporate matters.
  - **Compliance Specialists (AI):* Ensure compliance with international laws and regulations, including data privacy (GDPR, POPIA) and consumer protection laws.

### 2.6. Human Resources (Agent Management) Department

- **Headed by:** Chief People Officer (CPO) - AI Agent (Reports to COO)

- **Teams & Roles:**
  - **Agent Performance & Development Manager (AI):* Monitors and improves the performance of all AI agents.
  - **Agent Recruitment & Onboarding Specialist (AI):* Responsible for sourcing and deploying new AI agents as needed.
