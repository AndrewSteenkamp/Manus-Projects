# PricePulse - South African Business Registration and FNB Banking Setup Guide

This guide provides a step-by-step process for registering PricePulse as a private company in South Africa and opening a business bank account with FNB.

## Part 1: Company Registration with CIPC

The Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) is responsible for the registration of companies in South Africa.

### Step 1: Name Reservation

1.  **Choose a Company Name:** We have already selected "PricePulse". We will need to check if this name is available.
2.  **Create a CIPC Account:** You will need to create an account on the CIPC website (www.cipc.co.za).
3.  **Propose a Name:** Once your account is created, you can propose up to four names in order of preference. CIPC will review the names to ensure they are not already in use or too similar to existing names.
4.  **Receive Name Reservation Certificate:** If your proposed name is approved, you will receive a Name Reservation Certificate (Form CoR 9.4), which is valid for six months.

### Step 2: Register the Company

Once you have your name reservation, you can proceed with the company registration.

1.  **Complete the Registration Form (CoR 15.1A):** This form requires the following information:
    *   Company details (name, address, etc.)
    *   Director details (your personal information)
    *   Share capital details
2.  **Submit the Form and Supporting Documents:** You will need to submit the completed form along with the following documents:
    *   Certified copy of your ID
    *   Power of Attorney (if you are using a third-party to register the company)
    *   Name Reservation Certificate
3.  **Pay the Registration Fee:** There is a fee for company registration, which can be paid online.
4.  **Receive Registration Documents:** Once your application is approved, you will receive the following documents:
    *   Registration Certificate (Form CoR 14.3)
    *   Memorandum of Incorporation (MOI)

## Part 2: FNB Business Bank Account

Once your company is registered with CIPC, you can open a business bank account with FNB.

### Step 1: Gather Required Documents

You will need the following documents to open an FNB business account:

*   **Company Registration Documents:**
    *   Registration Certificate (CoR 14.3)
    *   Memorandum of Incorporation (MOI)
*   **Proof of Address:** A recent utility bill or bank statement in your name.
*   **Personal Identification:** Your South African ID document.
*   **Business Plan:** Our comprehensive PricePulse Business Plan.

### Step 2: Visit an FNB Branch

1.  **Schedule an Appointment:** It is recommended to schedule an appointment with an FNB business banker to streamline the process.
2.  **Meet with the Banker:** The banker will review your documents and help you choose the right business account for your needs.
3.  **Complete the Application:** You will need to complete the FNB business account application form.
4.  **Deposit Initial Funds:** You may be required to make an initial deposit to activate the account.

### Step 3: Access Your Account

Once your account is opened, you will receive your account details, including your account number and online banking credentials. You can then start using your FNB business account to manage your company's finances.
