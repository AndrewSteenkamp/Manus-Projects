# Siener Van Rensburg: Specific Visions and Their Predicted Meanings

**Research Focus:** Van Rensburg's actual visions with his interpretations and what he predicted would happen

## Major Visions and Predictions

### 1. The Dark Cloud from the East - Rise of Communism

**Vision:** Van Rensburg saw "a black cloud creeping over Europe from the east and covering it in darkness like night. A Russian emerges from the north. He is looking straight at us. The earth here (in South Africa) becomes like fallow land. But it is pitch black in Europe."

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** This vision had to do with the rise of Communism in Europe.

**Prediction:** 
- Communism would spread from Russia across Europe
- Europe would be covered in darkness (Communist control)
- This would affect South Africa as well

**Fulfillment Status:** Largely fulfilled - Soviet expansion across Eastern Europe after WWII

### 2. The Red Bulls Vision - Communist Expansion

**Vision (April 4, 1919):** "Three sickles appear one behind the other and stack one on top of the other until they become one. Then wagons trek from North to East; they look like Turkish wagons, but they are new and are covered with canvas."

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** The fall of Communism will be of short duration. Russia will return to its old ways of 'oppression, permanent revolution and dictatorship'. Shortly thereafter a man from the old Ottoman Empire (Hussein?) will ignite a new war in the Middle East.

**Prediction:**
- Communist symbols (sickles) would unite
- Russia would experience a brief fall of Communism but return to oppressive ways
- A Middle Eastern leader would start a new war
- Suicide bomber attacks would follow

**Fulfillment Status:** Partially fulfilled - Soviet Union fell but Russia returned to authoritarian rule; Middle East conflicts ongoing

### 3. The Terrified Woman in Black - Death and Destruction

**Vision (August 21, 1916):** "I see a terrified woman in black," indicating the death and destruction that Communism brings to the West.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** This represented the death and destruction that Communism would bring to Western nations.

**Prediction:** Communist ideology would bring death and destruction to Western countries.

### 4. Communism's Global Spread

**Vision (November 21, 1918):** "Communism will spread its evil influence over the world, causing chaos and misery."

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** Communism would become a global force causing widespread chaos.

**Prediction:** 
- Communism would spread worldwide
- It would cause chaos and misery globally
- This would be part of end-time events

### 5. Asylum-Seekers Invade Europe

**Vision:** Van Rensburg saw massive waves of asylum-seekers flooding into Europe.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** This would be part of Europe's downfall and transformation.

**Prediction:**
- Massive immigration waves would hit Europe
- European governments would be unable to handle the numbers
- This would contribute to Europe's destabilization

**Fulfillment Status:** Ongoing - European migrant crisis since 2015

### 6. The Rise of Islam

**Vision:** Van Rensburg saw the rise of Islamic power and influence.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** Islam would become a major world power again, challenging Western dominance.

**Prediction:**
- Islam would rise as a global force
- Islamic militants would challenge Western powers
- This would lead to religious warfare

**Fulfillment Status:** Ongoing - Rise of Islamic movements and conflicts

### 7. Terrorist Attack on England

**Vision (April 8, 1918):** "England's decision to support America in its war against the Ottoman Empire (Iraq?) will directly result in a devastating Muslim bomb attack on the British transport system. From now on sabotage and arson (explosives) will be the order of the day in the rest of Europe. He saw riots breaking out and Englishmen with immigrants among them — meaning those two groups will also clash. England will now be under constant attack."

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** England's involvement in Middle Eastern wars would result in terrorist attacks on British soil.

**Prediction:**
- Muslim bomb attacks on British transport system
- Sabotage and explosions throughout Europe
- Riots between English people and immigrants
- Constant attacks on England

**Fulfillment Status:** Fulfilled - London bombings (2005), various terrorist attacks

### 8. British Troops Forced Out of Iraq

**Vision:** Van Rensburg saw British forces being forced to withdraw from Iraq.

**Prediction:** "In the end the British troops will be forced to pull out of Iraq."

**Fulfillment Status:** Fulfilled - British withdrawal from Iraq

### 9. European Economic Collapse

**Vision (September 21, 1919):** "The capitalists find themselves in trouble. There is a serious shortage of money, no clothes, no food, no water. They will be powerless to do anything. It is going to be a very trying and dangerous time for everyone and people will be in great distress. There is talk of treason and bribery and very few who can be trusted during that time. No one will be able to help."

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** Europe would face severe economic collapse and social breakdown.

**Prediction:**
- Severe economic crisis in Europe
- Shortages of basic necessities
- Social breakdown and distrust
- Powerless leadership

### 10. War Escalation Vision

**Vision (August 8, 1918):** "The war escalates in Europe, the Middle East and then in the south. Again the Muslim Movement is involved; now their influence is also felt in South Africa."

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** A major war would spread from Europe to the Middle East and then to the southern hemisphere, including South Africa.

**Prediction:**
- War would start in Europe
- Spread to Middle East
- Eventually reach South Africa
- Muslim movements would be involved globally

### 11. The Great Depression Vision

**Vision (September 29, 1919):** "In Europe, there is a shop, but there is nobody inside it; people with wagons, carrying rubbish, flee to the south. In the northwest of Europe, many white-backed oxen appear (America?) and when the oxen disappear, little naked Blacks (African people) run to the north."

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** This referred to a great depression that would come over Europe, so terrible that England would lose everything, and thousands of hungry and destitute natives from Africa would flood Europe.

**Prediction:**
- Severe economic depression in Europe
- Mass migration from Africa to Europe
- European economic collapse
- Abandonment of European cities

**Fulfillment Status:** Partially fulfilled - European economic crises, African migration to Europe

### 12. World War III Predictions

**Vision:** Van Rensburg had multiple visions about a third world war.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** A devastating global conflict would begin in Europe and spread worldwide.

**Predictions:**
- World War III would start in Europe
- It would be more devastating than previous wars
- Russia would play a major role
- The war would spread globally
- It would involve religious/ideological conflicts

### 13. South African Predictions

**Vision:** Multiple visions about South Africa's future.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretations and Predictions:**
- Black majority rule would come to South Africa
- There would be a period of great trouble and bloodshed
- The Boer people would eventually regain some form of independence
- A remnant of the Boer people would survive the troubles
- The old Transvaal flag would fly again

**His Final Prediction (Deathbed):** "Ek sien die vierkleur weer wapper" (I see the four-color flag waving again)

### 14. Germany's Role

**Vision:** Van Rensburg saw Germany playing a crucial role in future events.

**Predictions:**
- Germany would be involved in major future conflicts
- Germany would face internal struggles
- Germany would be key to European events

### 15. America's Involvement

**Vision:** Van Rensburg saw America's role in global conflicts.

**Predictions:**
- America would be drawn into global conflicts
- America would face internal divisions
- America's power would be challenged

## Van Rensburg's Interpretation Methodology

Van Rensburg interpreted his symbolic visions through:

1. **Biblical Framework:** Using biblical symbolism and prophecy
2. **Color Symbolism:** Different colors represented different nations/ideologies
3. **Animal Symbolism:** Bulls, cattle, and other animals represented political forces
4. **Directional Symbolism:** East, West, North, South had specific meanings
5. **Repeated Contemplation:** He would see visions multiple times until he understood them

Van Rensburg's visions painted a picture of escalating global conflicts, the rise and fall of ideologies, massive population movements, and ultimately a period of great tribulation followed by restoration for his people.



## Additional Detailed Visions and Predictions

### 16. The Third World War Vision (1918)

**Vision:** "I see there will be a time when the whole world will be ploughed under. This will be the beginning of the Third – and last – World War and everything will be in confusion. Then I saw a snake lying on the ploughed land. I could not see its head or tail."

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** This would be the final global conflict that would devastate the entire world.

**Prediction:**
- A third and final world war would occur
- The entire world would be "ploughed under" (devastated)
- Everything would be in confusion
- The conflict would be so extensive its beginning and end would be unclear (snake with no visible head or tail)

### 17. Fulfilled Predictions - Historical Accuracy

**Van Rensburg's Documented Fulfilled Predictions:**

1. **The outcome of the Boer War** - Predicted Boer defeat
2. **The Great Flu epidemic of 1918** - Saw the global pandemic
3. **England's loss of all her colonies** - Predicted British Empire collapse
4. **Independence for Ireland** - Saw Irish independence before it happened
5. **The atomic disaster at Chernobyl on April 26 1986** - Predicted nuclear disaster
6. **Lady Di's death** - Predicted the divorce and tragic death of "a beautiful English Lady in a car accident who would be mourned by the whole world"
7. **Civil war in Bosnia** - Predicted Balkan conflicts
8. **Dr HF Verwoerd, former Prime Minister, will die at the hand of a close friend** - Predicted assassination
9. **The release of Nelson Mandela by ex-President FW de Klerk** - Predicted Mandela's release
10. **The Necklace Murders by blacks against blacks** - Predicted internal violence
11. **South Africa will be governed by a black government** - Predicted end of apartheid

### 18. Future Predictions - Not Yet Fulfilled

**Van Rensburg's Unfulfilled Predictions:**

1. **Japan will be destroyed by earthquakes** - Major seismic destruction of Japan

2. **Ethnical violence in Russia and Europe** - Racial conflicts across Europe and Russia

3. **Racial violence will explode worldwide** - Global racial conflicts at the turn of the century leading to World War III, during which Germany and America will fight side by side

4. **Laser weapons** - Van Rensburg predicted in 1920 the use of "terrible electrical rays that sow death and destruction from above and below, and soak the earth in blood"

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** These would be advanced weapons that could kill from great distances, possibly electromagnetic pulse (EMP) attacks that would knock out electrical grids.

### 19. The Sisal Plant Vision - Political Meetings

**Vision:** When the image of the Sisal plant occurred in his visions.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** This was a portent of an important meeting, assembly or parliament.

**Prediction:** Major political gatherings and assemblies would be significant markers of future events.

### 20. The Snake Vision - Hidden Conflicts

**Vision:** Multiple visions of snakes in various contexts.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** Snakes represented hidden enemies, deception, and conflicts whose true nature was concealed.

**Predictions:**
- Hidden conflicts would emerge
- Deceptive leaders would arise
- The true nature of global conflicts would be concealed from the public

### 21. The Ploughed Land Vision - Global Devastation

**Vision:** Seeing the entire world as ploughed land.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** This represented complete global devastation and the overturning of the existing world order.

**Prediction:**
- The current world system would be completely overturned
- Global devastation would precede a new world order
- No nation would escape the coming upheaval

### 22. Parliamentary Testimony (1916)

**Historical Validation:** In 1916, General Smuts (past Prime Minister of South Africa) and General J.B.M. Hertzog gave witness before a Parliamentary Committee regarding the truth of van Rensburg's visions.

**Their Testimony:** They confirmed the accuracy of his predictions and the practical value of his visions during military operations.

### 23. The Electrical Rays Vision (1920)

**Vision:** Van Rensburg saw "terrible electrical rays that sow death and destruction from above and below, and soak the earth in blood."

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** Future warfare would involve electrical/energy weapons that could kill from great distances.

**Prediction:**
- Advanced energy weapons would be developed
- These weapons would cause massive casualties
- They would attack from both air and ground
- The destruction would be unprecedented

**Modern Context:** This could refer to:
- Directed energy weapons
- EMP attacks

- Advanced electromagnetic warfare

### 24. The World Ploughed Under - Complete Reset

**Vision:** The entire world being "ploughed under" like a field.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** The existing world order would be completely destroyed and remade.

**Prediction:**
- Complete collapse of current political systems

- A new world order would emerge from the devastation

### 25. Germany and America Alliance

**Vision:** Van Rensburg saw Germany and America fighting side by side.

**Van Rensburg's Interpretation:** Despite historical conflicts, these nations would become allies in the final global conflict.

**Prediction:**
- Germany and America would form a military alliance

## Van Rensburg's Symbolic Interpretation System

### Color Symbolism
- **Red:** Communist/Socialist forces
- **Black:** Death, destruction, or African peoples
- **White:** European/Western peoples or purity
- **Blue:** Often represented water or spiritual forces

### Animal Symbolism
- **Bulls:** Political/military forces (different colors = different nations)
- **Snakes:** Hidden enemies, deception, unclear situations
- **Cattle:** Peoples or nations being led
- **Horses:** Military forces or rapid movement

### Directional Symbolism
- **East:** Communist/Asian forces
- **West:** European/American forces
- **North:** Often Russia or cold/harsh forces
- **South:** Africa or warm/friendly forces

### Plant Symbolism
- **Sisal plants:** Important political meetings or assemblies
- **Trees:** Nations or established powers
- **Ploughed land:** Destruction and renewal
