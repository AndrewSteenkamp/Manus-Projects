## 1. Overview

### Purpose

This manual outlines the comprehensive vetting and recruitment process for service providers on the South African Home Services Marketplace. The vetting process is the cornerstone of the platform's value proposition, ensuring that only qualified, trustworthy, and professional service providers are connected with customers.

### Key Principles

**Trust First:** Every provider must undergo rigorous background checks and qualification verification before accepting their first job.

**Continuous Quality:** Vetting is not a one-time event. Providers are continuously monitored through customer feedback, job completion rates, and periodic re-verification.

**Compliance:** All vetting procedures comply with South African law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), labour laws, and industry-specific regulations.

**Transparency:** Providers understand exactly what is required and why. Customers can see verification badges and understand what they mean.

## 2. Provider Application Process

### 2.1 Application Form

Providers complete an online application form accessible at `[platform-url]/provider/apply`. The form collects:

#### Personal Information
- Full legal name (as per ID document)
- South African ID number
- Date of birth
- Contact details (phone, email, WhatsApp)
- Physical address with proof of residence

#### Business Information
- Business name (if applicable)
- Business registration number (if registered)
- Years of experience in trade
- Primary service category (plumbing, electrical, handyman, etc.)
- Service areas (suburbs/regions willing to travel to)

#### Professional Qualifications
- Trade certificates or diplomas
- Professional licenses (e.g., electrical contractor license)
- Professional body memberships (e.g., IOPSA for plumbers)
- Continuing education or specialized training

#### Insurance and Legal
- Public liability insurance details (policy number, provider, coverage amount)
- Professional indemnity insurance (if applicable)
- Tax clearance status
- Criminal record disclosure

#### Portfolio
- Upload minimum 5 photos of previous work
- Provide 3 professional references (name, contact, relationship)
- Brief description of most complex project completed

#### Consent and Declarations
- Consent to background check
- Consent to qualification verification
- Declaration that all information provided is accurate
- Agreement to platform terms of service

### 2.2 Application Review Timeline

| Stage | Timeline | Responsible Party |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| Application submission | Day 0 | Provider |
| Initial review | Day 1 | Platform admin |
| Background check initiation | Day 2 | Platform admin + screening partner |
| Qualification verification | Day 2-5 | Platform admin |
| Interview scheduling | Day 5 | Platform admin |
| Interview completion | Day 7 | Platform admin |
| Final decision | Day 10 | Platform admin |
| Onboarding | Day 11-12 | Platform admin + provider |

**Target: Complete vetting within 10-12 business days**

## 3. Vetting Checklist

### 3.1 Document Verification Checklist

Use this checklist for every provider application:

#### Identity Verification
- [ ] Valid South African ID document submitted
- [ ] ID number verified against Home Affairs database (if accessible)
- [ ] Proof of residence submitted (utility bill, bank statement <3 months old)
- [ ] Address matches ID and proof of residence

#### Business Registration (if applicable)
- [ ] CIPC registration certificate submitted
- [ ] Business name matches registration
- [ ] Business is in good standing (no deregistration)
- [ ] Tax clearance certificate (if required for category)

#### Qualifications and Licenses
- [ ] Trade certificates submitted and legible
- [ ] Certificates verified with issuing institution
- [ ] Professional licenses current and valid
- [ ] Professional body membership confirmed (if applicable)

#### Insurance
- [ ] Public liability insurance policy submitted
- [ ] Policy is current (not expired)
- [ ] Coverage meets minimum requirements (R1M recommended)
- [ ] Insurance provider is reputable and registered
- [ ] Professional indemnity insurance (for specialized trades)

#### References
- [ ] Minimum 3 references provided
- [ ] References contacted and verified
- [ ] References provide positive feedback
- [ ] At least 1 reference is from a recent client (within 12 months)

#### Portfolio
- [ ] Minimum 5 work photos submitted
- [ ] Photos show quality workmanship
- [ ] Photos appear authentic (not stock images)
- [ ] Work aligns with claimed expertise

#### Background Check
- [ ] Criminal background check completed
- [ ] No disqualifying offenses (theft, fraud, violence)
- [ ] Credit check completed (optional, for financial services)
- [ ] Social media review completed (public profiles only)

#### Interview
- [ ] Video interview conducted
- [ ] Communication skills assessed
- [ ] Technical knowledge verified
- [ ] Professionalism and reliability assessed

### 3.2 Disqualifying Factors

Automatic rejection if any of the following apply:

**Criminal History:**
- Conviction for theft, burglary, or property crimes
- Conviction for fraud or financial crimes
- Conviction for violent crimes
- Outstanding warrants or pending criminal cases

**Professional Issues:**
- Falsified qualifications or licenses
- Expired or invalid licenses for regulated trades
- No valid insurance for high-risk trades
- Multiple negative references

**Integrity Concerns:**
- Providing false information on application
- Refusal to consent to background checks
- Unprofessional conduct during interview
- Evidence of discriminatory behavior or hate speech

## 4. Background Check Procedures

### 4.1 Partner Selection

**Recommended South African Background Check Providers:**

| Provider | Services | Cost per Check | Turnaround Time |
|----------|----------|----------------|-----------------|
| **iFacts** | Criminal, credit, qualification verification | R150-300 | 3-5 business days |
| **Managed Integrity Evaluation (MIE)** | Comprehensive screening | R200-350 | 3-7 business days |
| **Lexis Nexis** | Criminal, identity, credit | R180-320 | 2-4 business days |

**Selection Criteria:**
- POPIA compliant
- Registered with Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority (PSIRA) if applicable
- Access to South African Police Service (SAPS) criminal records
- Reasonable turnaround time (under 7 days)
- Cost-effective for volume screening

### 4.2 Background Check Components

#### Criminal Record Check
- Search SAPS criminal records database
- Check for convictions in past 10 years
- Focus on relevant offenses (theft, fraud, violence)
- Verify no outstanding warrants

#### Identity Verification
- Confirm ID number is valid and matches applicant
- Check for identity fraud indicators
- Verify ID has not been reported stolen

#### Credit Check (Optional)
- For providers handling expensive equipment or deposits
- Check for judgments, defaults, or insolvency
- Not a disqualifier unless severe financial distress

#### Qualification Verification
- Verify certificates with issuing institutions
- Check professional body memberships
- Confirm licenses with regulatory authorities

### 4.3 POPIA Compliance

**Consent Requirements:**
- Explicit written consent from provider
- Clear explanation of what will be checked and why
- Consent form includes right to access information
- Consent form includes right to dispute inaccuracies

**Data Protection:**
- Background check results stored securely (encrypted)
- Access limited to authorized platform administrators
- Results not shared with third parties
- Results retained only as long as necessary

**Provider Rights:**
- Right to access their background check results
- Right to dispute inaccurate information
- Right to request deletion after leaving platform
- Right to explanation if rejected based on background check

## 5. Qualification Verification

### 5.1 Trade-Specific Requirements

#### Electricians

**Required Qualifications:**
- National Certificate in Electrical Engineering (N1-N3) or equivalent
- Trade Test Certificate (Wireman's License)
- Registration with Department of Employment and Labour

**Verification Process:**
- Contact Department of Employment and Labour to verify registration
- Verify trade test certificate authenticity
- Check for any suspensions or disciplinary actions

**Additional Checks:**
- Membership with Electrical Contractors Association of South Africa (ECASA) - preferred but not required
- Specialized certifications (e.g., solar PV installation, SANS 10142-1 compliance)

#### Plumbers

**Required Qualifications:**
- Plumbing trade certificate or diploma
- Plumber's license (if operating independently)
- Membership with Institute of Plumbing South Africa (IOPSA) - preferred

**Verification Process:**
- Verify trade certificate with issuing institution
- Check IOPSA membership if claimed
- Verify municipal registration if required in service area

**Additional Checks:**
- Gas installation certification (if offering gas services)
- Water efficiency certification
- Backflow prevention certification

#### Handymen/General Maintenance

**Required Qualifications:**
- No formal qualification required, but preferred:
  - Carpentry, painting, or general maintenance certificates
  - Apprenticeship completion
  - Manufacturer training (e.g., furniture assembly)

**Verification Process:**
- Verify any claimed certificates
- Heavy emphasis on portfolio and references
- Practical skills assessment through interview

**Additional Checks:**
- Specialized skills (tiling, drywall, painting)
- Tool ownership and maintenance
- Vehicle reliability (for mobile service)

#### HVAC/Air Conditioning Specialists

**Required Qualifications:**
- Refrigeration and Air Conditioning trade certificate
- ODP (Ozone Depleting Substances) certificate (mandatory)
- Electrical qualification (for installations)

**Verification Process:**
- Verify ODP certificate (legal requirement)
- Check trade certificate authenticity
- Verify electrical qualifications if doing wiring

**Additional Checks:**
- Manufacturer certifications (Daikin, LG, Samsung, etc.)
- Heat pump installation experience
- Energy efficiency knowledge

#### Solar Power Installers

**Required Qualifications:**
- Electrical trade qualification (mandatory)
- Solar PV installation certification
- SANS 10142-1 compliance knowledge

**Verification Process:**
- Verify electrical qualifications
- Check solar-specific training certificates
- Verify experience with grid-tied and off-grid systems

**Additional Checks:**
- Battery system installation experience
- Inverter programming knowledge
- Structural assessment capability (roof mounting)

### 5.2 Verification Methods

**Direct Institutional Contact:**
- Email or call issuing institution with provider consent
- Provide certificate number and provider details
- Request confirmation of authenticity and issue date

**Online Verification Systems:**
- Use online verification portals where available
- Department of Employment and Labour online register
- Professional body membership databases

**Document Analysis:**
- Check for security features (watermarks, holograms)
- Verify issuing institution details are correct
- Check for signs of tampering or forgery

## 6. Interview and Assessment

### 6.1 Video Interview Structure

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes  
**Format:** Video call (Zoom, Google Meet, or WhatsApp video)  
**Interviewer:** Platform administrator or designated interviewer

#### Interview Sections

**Section 1: Introduction and Background (5 minutes)**
- Introduce platform and interview purpose
- Ask provider to introduce themselves
- Discuss years of experience and how they got into the trade

**Section 2: Technical Knowledge Assessment (15 minutes)**
- Ask trade-specific technical questions (see below)
- Discuss approach to common problems
- Assess troubleshooting methodology

**Section 3: Customer Service and Professionalism (10 minutes)**
- Scenario: Difficult customer situation - how would they handle it?
- Discuss communication style and customer expectations
- Ask about handling complaints or mistakes

**Section 4: Business Practices (10 minutes)**
- Discuss pricing methodology
- Ask about availability and response times
- Discuss tool and equipment maintenance
- Ask about insurance and safety practices

**Section 5: Platform Expectations (5 minutes)**
- Explain platform commission structure
- Discuss job acceptance and completion expectations
- Answer provider questions about platform

### 6.2 Trade-Specific Technical Questions

#### Electricians
1. "A customer reports that their circuit breaker keeps tripping. Walk me through your diagnostic process."
2. "What are the key requirements of SANS 10142-1 for residential electrical installations?"
3. "How do you ensure electrical safety when working in an occupied home?"
4. "Explain the difference between single-phase and three-phase power and when each is used."

#### Plumbers
1. "A customer has a persistent leak under their kitchen sink. Describe your troubleshooting steps."
2. "What are the most common causes of low water pressure in a home?"
3. "How do you prevent water hammer in a plumbing system?"
4. "Explain the importance of proper drainage slope and how you ensure it."

#### Handymen
1. "A customer needs several small repairs (leaky tap, squeaky door, loose tile). How do you prioritize and price this?"
2. "What tools do you consider essential for general handyman work?"
3. "How do you handle a job that turns out to be more complex than initially assessed?"

#### HVAC Specialists
1. "A customer's air conditioner is running but not cooling. What are the most likely causes?"
2. "Explain the importance of proper refrigerant handling and your ODP certification."
3. "How do you size an air conditioning unit for a room or home?"

#### Solar Installers
1. "Walk me through the process of designing a solar system for a typical 3-bedroom home."
2. "What are the key considerations for roof mounting solar panels?"
3. "How do you ensure compliance with municipal and Eskom requirements for grid-tied systems?"

### 6.3 Assessment Scoring

Rate each provider on the following criteria (1-5 scale):

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Technical Knowledge** | 30% | Demonstrates solid understanding of trade principles and practices |
| **Communication Skills** | 25% | Communicates clearly, professionally, and in a customer-friendly manner |
| **Problem-Solving** | 20% | Shows logical troubleshooting approach and adaptability |
| **Professionalism** | 15% | Punctual, well-presented, respectful, and reliable |
| **Customer Service Orientation** | 10% | Demonstrates care for customer satisfaction and quality work |

**Minimum Passing Score:** 3.5/5.0 average

**Red Flags:**
- Unable to answer basic technical questions
- Dismissive or arrogant attitude
- Poor communication skills
- Unrealistic pricing or availability claims
- Unwilling to follow platform guidelines

## 7. Onboarding Process

### 7.1 Approval and Welcome

Once a provider passes all vetting stages:

**Day 1: Approval Notification**
- Send approval email with welcome message
- Provide login credentials to provider dashboard
- Share onboarding checklist and timeline

**Day 2: Platform Training**
- Schedule 30-minute onboarding call
- Walk through provider dashboard features
- Explain job acceptance, quoting, and completion process
- Demonstrate in-app messaging and customer communication

**Day 3: Profile Setup**
- Provider completes detailed profile
- Uploads professional photo
- Writes service description and specializations
- Sets service areas and availability

**Day 4: Payment Setup**
- Provider adds bank account details for payouts
- Explains commission structure and payment timeline
- Reviews transaction fees and payment processing

**Day 5: Go Live**
- Profile reviewed and approved by admin
- Provider marked as "Active - Probationary"
- Provider can now receive job requests

### 7.2 Onboarding Materials

Provide providers with:

**Digital Welcome Pack:**
- Platform user guide (PDF)
- Best practices for winning jobs (tips on quoting, communication, professionalism)
- Customer service guidelines
- Safety and compliance reminders
- FAQ document

**Video Tutorials:**
- How to use the provider dashboard
- How to submit competitive quotes
- How to communicate with customers
- How to handle disputes or issues

**Support Contacts:**
- Provider support email
- Provider support WhatsApp number
- Emergency contact for urgent issues

## 8. Recruitment Strategy

### 8.1 Target Provider Profile

**Ideal Provider Characteristics:**
- 3+ years of trade experience
- Valid qualifications and licenses
- Own tools and reliable transport
- Professional attitude and appearance
- Good communication skills
- Smartphone with internet access (for app usage)
- Availability for at least 20 hours per week

**Geographic Targeting:**
- Phase 1: Cape Town (Northern Suburbs, Southern Suburbs, City Bowl, Atlantic Seaboard)
- Phase 2: Johannesburg (Sandton, Rosebank, Randburg, Fourways)
- Phase 3: Durban (Umhlanga, Durban North, Westville, Berea)

### 8.2 Recruitment Channels

#### Channel 1: Direct Outreach (Highest Quality)

**Trade Supply Stores:**
- Visit Builders Warehouse, Chamberlain, Timbercity, Voltex, etc.
- Distribute flyers and business cards
- Speak directly with tradespeople shopping for supplies
- Offer on-site registration assistance

**Trade Associations and Bodies:**
- Contact IOPSA (Institute of Plumbing South Africa)
- Contact ECASA (Electrical Contractors Association)
- Attend trade association meetings and events
- Offer group presentations

**Trade Schools and Training Centers:**
- Partner with TVET colleges
- Offer platform access to recent graduates
- Provide mentorship opportunities with experienced providers

#### Channel 2: Digital Marketing

**Facebook and Instagram Ads:**
- Target: Males 25-55, Cape Town, interests: construction, home improvement, small business
- Ad creative: "Get More Clients - Join [Platform Name]"
- Landing page: Provider application with benefits highlighted

**Google Ads:**
- Keywords: "get plumbing clients", "find handyman jobs", "electrician leads Cape Town"
- Landing page: Provider benefits and application form

**WhatsApp Business:**
- Create WhatsApp Business account for provider inquiries
- Quick response templates for common questions
- Personal follow-up with interested providers

**LinkedIn:**
- Target registered businesses and sole proprietors
- Sponsored posts highlighting provider success stories
- Direct outreach to qualified professionals

#### Channel 3: Referral Program

**Provider Referral Incentive:**
- Existing providers earn R500 for each qualified provider they refer
- Referred provider must complete 5 jobs successfully
- Bonus paid after 5th job completion

**Customer Referral:**
- Customers can recommend their trusted tradespeople
- Recommended providers get fast-tracked vetting
- Customer receives R100 credit when provider completes first job

#### Channel 4: Community Partnerships

**Property Management Companies:**
- Partner with property managers who need reliable contractors
- Offer platform access to their preferred providers
- Provide bulk onboarding support

**Real Estate Agencies:**
- Partner with agents who recommend service providers to new homeowners
- Offer co-marketing opportunities
- Provide agent referral incentives

**Homeowner Associations:**
- Present platform at HOA meetings
- Offer group discounts for association members
- Recruit providers who already serve the community

### 8.3 Recruitment Targets

**Phase 1 (Month 1-2): Cape Town Launch**
- Target: 50 vetted providers

## 9. Provider Tiers and Progression

### 9.1 Tier Structure

#### Tier 1: Probationary (First 5 Jobs)

**Badge:** "New Provider"  
**Commission:** 20%  
**Benefits:**
- Access to platform and job requests
- Basic profile listing
- Standard customer support

**Requirements:**
- Complete all vetting requirements
- Maintain 3.0+ star rating
- Complete jobs within agreed timeframe

**Progression:** Automatically upgrade to Verified after 5 completed jobs with 4.0+ average rating

#### Tier 2: Verified (5+ Jobs, 4.0+ Rating)

**Badge:** "Verified Provider"  
**Commission:** 18%  
**Benefits:**
- Reduced commission rate
- Priority listing in search results
- Access to provider training resources
- Quarterly performance reports

**Requirements:**
- Maintain 4.0+ star rating
- <5% job cancellation rate
- <3% dispute rate
- Complete at least 2 jobs per month

**Progression:** Upgrade to Premium after 50 completed jobs with 4.5+ average rating

#### Tier 3: Premium (50+ Jobs, 4.5+ Rating)

**Badge:** "Premium Provider"  
**Commission:** 15%  
**Benefits:**
- Lowest commission rate
- Top placement in search results
- Featured provider opportunities
- Dedicated account manager
- Priority customer support
- Access to exclusive high-value jobs
- Marketing support (featured in blog posts, social media)

**Requirements:**
- Maintain 4.5+ star rating
- <2% job cancellation rate
- <1% dispute rate
- Complete at least 10 jobs per month
- Respond to quotes within 2 hours

**Progression:** Maintain Premium status through continued excellence

### 9.2 Tier Demotion

Providers can be demoted if performance declines:

**Demotion Triggers:**
- Rating falls below tier minimum for 30 consecutive days
- Excessive job cancellations (>5% rate)
- Multiple customer complaints or disputes
- Failure to respond to job requests within 24 hours
- Violation of platform terms of service

**Demotion Process:**
1. Warning email with performance concerns
2. 14-day improvement period
3. If no improvement, demotion to lower tier
4. Provider notified of demotion and new commission rate

### 9.3 Provider Suspension and Removal

**Suspension (Temporary):**
- Serious customer complaint under investigation
- Suspected fraud or policy violation
- Insurance or license expiration (until renewed)
- Provider request (vacation, personal leave)

**Permanent Removal:**
- Confirmed fraud or dishonesty
- Multiple serious customer complaints
- Criminal activity
- Repeated policy violations
- Consistently poor performance despite warnings

## 10. Continuous Monitoring

### 10.1 Performance Metrics

Track the following metrics for each provider:

**Quality Metrics:**
- Average customer rating (1-5 stars)
- Number of 5-star reviews
- Number of 1-2 star reviews
- Customer complaint rate
- Dispute rate

**Reliability Metrics:**
- Job acceptance rate (% of quotes that lead to bookings)
- Job completion rate (% of accepted jobs completed)
- Job cancellation rate
- On-time arrival rate
- Response time to job requests

**Activity Metrics:**
- Jobs completed per month
- Total earnings
- Active days per month
- Quote submission rate

### 10.2 Automated Monitoring Systems

**Real-Time Alerts:**
- Alert admin if provider receives 2-star or lower rating
- Alert admin if provider cancels 2+ jobs in 7 days
- Alert admin if provider has 3+ unresolved disputes
- Alert admin if provider's insurance or license expires

**Weekly Reports:**
- Top-performing providers (for recognition and rewards)
- Underperforming providers (for intervention)
- New providers needing check-in
- Providers approaching tier progression

**Monthly Reviews:**
- Comprehensive performance review for all providers
- Identify providers for promotion or demotion
- Identify providers needing additional training or support
- Analyze trends (seasonal demand, service category performance)

### 10.3 Re-Verification Schedule

**Annual Re-Verification:**
- Confirm insurance is still valid and current
- Verify licenses have been renewed
- Check for any new criminal records
- Review portfolio and update profile

**Trigger-Based Re-Verification:**
- After serious customer complaint
- After dispute or quality concern
- If provider has been inactive for 6+ months
- If provider requests to add new service categories

### 10.4 Provider Support and Development

**Ongoing Training:**
- Quarterly webinars on platform best practices
- Customer service training
- Technical skills workshops (in partnership with suppliers)
- Business management tips (pricing, scheduling, customer retention)

**Provider Community:**
- Private Facebook group or WhatsApp group for providers
- Peer-to-peer support and advice
- Platform updates and announcements
- Recognition of top performers

**Feedback Loop:**
- Regular surveys asking for provider feedback
- Suggestion box for platform improvements
- Provider advisory council (top providers give input on policies)
