## 🌅 MORNING ROUTINE (10 minutes)

### Step 1: Check Your Phone 📱
- Open your email
- Look for the subject line: "Daily News Summary Ready"
- Click the link in the email
- **That's it!** The AI already found today's news for you

### Step 2: Copy & Paste Magic ✨
1. **Copy** the news summary (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C)
2. Go to **NotebookLM.google.com**
3. **Paste** it in the box (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V)
4. Click the big **"Generate"** button
5. Wait 2 minutes ⏰
6. **Download** the audio file

### Step 3: Upload to YouTube 🎬
1. Go to **YouTube.com/upload**
2. **Drag** the audio file into the box
3. **Copy** the title from your email
4. **Paste** it as your video title
5. **Copy** the description from your email
6. **Paste** it as your description
7. Click **"Publish"**

**🎉 DONE! Your video is live!**
