## Recommended Strategy: Hybrid Content Model

### 1. Keep What's Working
- Continue your successful long-form expert interviews (2-3 per week)
- Maintain relationships with Scott Ritter, Prof. Mearsheimer, Col MacGregor, etc.
- Keep your professional branding and visual identity

### 2. Add Daily Insights Format
- Launch daily 5-10 minute geopolitical briefings using NotebookLM
- Focus on breaking news, trend analysis, and quick takes
- Use automation tools to research and script daily content

### 3. Content Calendar Structure
**Monday/Wednesday/Friday:** Long-form expert interviews (current format)
**Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday/Sunday:** Daily insights briefings (new format)

This gives you 7 uploads per week instead of 2-3, dramatically improving algorithm performance.
