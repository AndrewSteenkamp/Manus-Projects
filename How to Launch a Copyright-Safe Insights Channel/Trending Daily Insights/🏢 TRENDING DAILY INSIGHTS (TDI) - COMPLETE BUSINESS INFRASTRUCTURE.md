## 🇿🇦 SOUTH AFRICAN BUSINESS SETUP

### Phase 1: Legal Entity Formation
**Company Type:** Private Company (Pty Ltd)
**Recommended Name:** Trending Daily Insights (Pty) Ltd
**Registration Process:**
1. **CIPC Registration** - Companies and Intellectual Property Commission
2. **Tax Registration** - SARS (South African Revenue Service)
3. **VAT Registration** - If turnover exceeds R1 million annually
4. **UIF Registration** - Unemployment Insurance Fund
5. **Workers' Compensation** - Department of Employment and Labour

### Phase 2: Banking Setup (FNB)
**Account Type:** FNB Business Account
**Requirements:**
- Company registration documents
- CIPC certificate
- Tax clearance certificate
- Proof of business address
- Director identification

**Payment Processing Solutions:**
- **FNB Merchant Services** - Local card processing
- **PayFast** - South African payment gateway
- **Stripe Atlas** - International payment processing
- **Wise Business** - Multi-currency accounts
- **Payoneer** - Global freelancer payments

### Phase 3: Compliance Framework
**Monthly Obligations:**
- VAT returns (if registered)
- PAYE submissions
- UIF contributions
- Workers' compensation returns

**Annual Obligations:**
- Annual return to CIPC
- Income tax returns
- Financial statements
- Audit (if required)
