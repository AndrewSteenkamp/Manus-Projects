### Recent Video Performance
- Views range from 13 to 353 views per video
- Most videos are 3-4 weeks old

### Current Challenges
1. **Low view counts** relative to subscriber base (13-353 views vs 2,250 subs)
2. **Long video format** may limit reach and retention
3. **Inconsistent upload schedule** (gaps between content)
4. **Limited content variety** (mostly interview format)

## Optimization Opportunities

### 1. Content Format Diversification
- Add shorter daily briefing videos (5-10 minutes) using NotebookLM
- Keep long-form interviews but supplement with daily insights
- Create "Quick Takes" on breaking news

### 2. Upload Consistency
- Implement daily upload schedule for short insights
- Weekly long-form interviews
- Use automation tools for consistent content creation

### 3. SEO and Discoverability
- Optimize titles for trending keywords
- Improve thumbnail designs for higher CTR
- Better video descriptions and tags

### 4. Audience Engagement
- Shorter videos will improve retention rates
- Daily content will increase channel activity
- More frequent uploads will improve algorithm performance

## Recommended Strategy

### Phase 1: Hybrid Approach (Immediate)
- Continue current long-form interview format (2-3 per week)

- Use automation tools to research and script daily content

### Phase 2: Content Optimization (30 days)

### Phase 3: Growth Acceleration (60-90 days)
