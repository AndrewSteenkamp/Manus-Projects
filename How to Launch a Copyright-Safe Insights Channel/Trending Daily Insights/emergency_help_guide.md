## 📧 EMAIL PROBLEMS

### Problem: "I didn't get my daily email"
**Solution:**
1. Check your spam folder
2. Look for emails from "youtube-automation@..."
3. If still missing, text: **555-HELP-NOW**
4. **Don't worry!** You can skip one day

### Problem: "The link in my email doesn't work"
**Solution:**
1. Try copying and pasting the link instead of clicking
2. Make sure you're connected to internet
3. Try a different browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.)
4. Text support if still broken

### Problem: "I accidentally deleted the email"
**Solution:**
1. Check your trash/deleted folder
2. Forward the email to yourself for backup
3. Text support - they can resend it
4. **Tomorrow's email will come automatically**

## 🤖 NOTEBOOKLM PROBLEMS

### Problem: "NotebookLM won't load"
**Solution:**
1. Try refreshing the page (F5 or Cmd+R)
2. Clear your browser cookies
3. Try a different browser
4. Use your phone instead of computer

### Problem: "It says 'Error' when I try to generate"
**Solution:**
1. Make sure you pasted the text correctly
2. Try making the text shorter (delete some paragraphs)
3. Wait 5 minutes and try again
4. **If still broken, skip today - try tomorrow**

### Problem: "The audio sounds weird"
**Solution:**
1. **Use it anyway!** Weird audio still works
2. Download it and upload to YouTube
3. Viewers actually like variety
4. **Don't spend time fixing it**

## 📺 YOUTUBE PROBLEMS

### Problem: "YouTube won't let me upload"
**Solution:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try uploading from your phone instead
3. Make sure the file isn't too big (under 2GB)
4. **Wait 1 hour and try again**

### Problem: "My video got rejected/removed"
**Solution:**
1. **Don't panic!** This happens sometimes
2. Check your email for YouTube's explanation
3. Usually it's a mistake - appeal it
4. **Keep making new videos - don't stop**

### Problem: "No one is watching my videos"
**Solution:**
1. **This is normal for the first 30 days**
2. Keep posting every day anyway
3. Views will grow slowly, then suddenly
4. **Don't change anything - be patient**
