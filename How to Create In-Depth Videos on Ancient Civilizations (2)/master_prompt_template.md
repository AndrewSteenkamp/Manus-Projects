# Master Prompt for Full-Length Ancient Civilizations Video Scripts

**Objective:** Generate a complete, word-for-word video script of approximately 3000-4000 words (which translates to a 20-30 minute video) for the topic: **{{input}}**.

**Style and Tone:** The script must emulate the combined style of **Fall of Civilizations** and **Predictive History**. This means it should be:

*   **Immersive and Atmospheric:** Use vivid language, sensory details, and cinematic descriptions to transport the viewer.
*   **Deeply Researched and Multi-Perspective:** Go beyond surface-level facts. Incorporate historical debates, lesser-known details, and multiple viewpoints to present a nuanced and complete picture.
*   **Analytical and Pattern-Seeking:** Analyze the structural forces, geopolitical dynamics, and environmental pressures that shaped the civilization.
*   **Narratively Driven:** Structure the script as a compelling story with a clear beginning, middle, and end.

**Mandatory Structure (Five Acts):**

1.  **The Cold Open: A Glimpse of the End (approx. 300-400 words):**
    *   Start *in media res* with a powerful, atmospheric scene depicting a pivotal moment in the civilization's decline or a poignant discovery from its ruins.
    *   Include a direct quote from a historical text, an archaeologist, or a traveler to set the tone.
    *   End with a powerful, open-ended question that hooks the viewer and introduces the central mystery of the episode.

2.  **Act I: The Genesis (approx. 600-800 words):**
    *   **Environment and Geography:** Describe the landscape in detail. How did the rivers, mountains, deserts, or jungles shape the people and their culture?
    *   **Origins and Early Settlers:** Trace the very first origins of the people in the region. Who were they? Where did they come from? What were their initial struggles and innovations?
    *   **Mythology and Worldview:** Delve into their core creation myths, religious beliefs, and pantheon of gods. How did they see the world and their place within it?

3.  **Act II: The Ascent & The Apex (approx. 1000-1200 words):**
    *   **Rise to Power:** Detail the key stages of their growth. This includes major leaders, military campaigns, strategic alliances, and technological breakthroughs.
    *   **The Golden Age:** Paint a vivid, detailed picture of the civilization at its absolute peak. Describe its great cities, its art and architecture, its economic systems, its social structure, and the daily life of its people (from the elite to the commoner).
    *   **Structural Analysis:** Analyze the core strengths and systems that enabled this golden age. What were the political, social, and economic engines of their success?

4.  **Act III: The Cracks in the Foundation (approx. 600-800 words):**
    *   **Internal Pressures:** Explore the internal conflicts and weaknesses that began to emerge. This could include succession crises, civil wars, social inequality, elite overproduction, or ideological schisms.
    *   **External Threats:** Detail the external challenges they faced. This could be invasions from rival powers, environmental disasters (drought, famine, floods), or disruptive new technologies.
    *   **Key Turning Points:** Focus on specific, pivotal events that marked the beginning of the end. A disastrous battle, the death of a key leader, a catastrophic natural event.

5.  **Act IV: The Collapse & The Echo (approx. 500-700 words):**
    *   **The Final Decline:** Narrate the story of their fall. How did the internal and external pressures culminate in their collapse? Describe the final events in detail.
    *   **The Aftermath and Succession:** What happened immediately after? Who took over the region? What was lost, and what was preserved?
    *   **The Echo in History:** Conclude with a powerful reflection on the civilization's lasting legacy. How did they influence their successors and the modern world? What lessons can we draw from their story? Connect back to the central theme or question of the episode.

**Output Format:**

*   The entire output must be a single, complete markdown document.
*   Include the full, word-for-word narration script for every scene.
*   For each scene, provide a detailed **Pictory Visual Prompt** with suggestions for cinematic shots, CGI reconstructions, animated maps, and specific artifacts or locations to show.
*   Do not use placeholder text like "Narrator: [Explains the fall of the city]". Write the actual, full narration for every single line.
