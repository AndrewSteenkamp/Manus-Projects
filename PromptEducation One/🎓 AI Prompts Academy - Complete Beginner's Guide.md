## 📦 Course Bundles Explained

### **What Are Bundles?**
Bundles are **packages of related courses sold together at a discount**. Think of it like buying a "meal deal" instead of individual items.

### **Why Use Bundles?**
1. **Customers save money** - They get more courses for less
2. **You sell more** - Instead of selling 1 course for R5, you sell 15 courses for R5
3. **Higher value** - Customers feel they're getting a great deal

### **How Bundles Work:**

**Example: E-Commerce Master Bundle**
- Contains: 15 e-commerce courses
- Individual price if bought separately: R7.15
- Bundle price: R5.00
- Customer saves: R2.15 (30%)

**When a customer buys a bundle:**
1. They pay once for the bundle
2. They get instant access to ALL courses in that bundle
3. They can watch all the courses as many times as they want

- Credit/debit cards
- Instant EFT
- SnapScan
- Zapper
- And more

### **Why PayFast?**
- **South African** - Built for SA businesses
- **Low fees** - Competitive transaction fees
- **Trusted** - Used by thousands of SA businesses
- **Easy** - Simple to integrate
