## Key Facebook Advertising Terms

- **Add To Cart (ATC)**: The number of add-to-cart events tracked by the Facebook Pixel that can be attributed to your ads.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: The average dollar value of each order or sale made on your website. Calculated by dividing total revenue by total number of orders.
- **Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO)**: An option that allows Facebook to automatically optimize the distribution of your campaign’s budget across all the ad sets in your campaign.
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**: The percentage of people who saw your ad and then clicked on it. Calculated by dividing total clicks by total impressions.
- **Conversion Rate (CVR)**: The percentage of ad clicks that converted to a sale. Calculated by dividing total sales (conversions) by total clicks.
- **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)**: The process of optimizing your ads to provide more conversions.
- **Cost Per Click (CPC)**: The average cost of one click on your Facebook ad.
- **Cost Per Engagement (CPE)**: The average cost of a user engaging with your ad. Calculated by dividing total ad spend by total number of engagements.
- **Cost Per Thousand Impressions (CPM)**: The cost for one thousand impressions of an ad.
- **Cost Per Action (CPA)**: The average cost of a visitor performing an action on your ad. Calculated by dividing ad spend by total number of actions performed.
- **Cost Per Purchase (CPP)**: The average cost of a purchase driven through your ads. Calculated by dividing total ad spend by total number of purchases.
- **Cross-Sell (XS)**: A sales technique to encourage a customer to purchase a related or complementary product.
- **Custom Audience (CA)**: A type of audience created for Facebook advertising campaigns using customer data (e.g., email addresses or phone numbers).
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV / LTV)**: A prediction of the total lifetime profit that can be attributed to a specific customer.
- **Down-sell (DS)**: A sales technique offering a cheaper product when a customer is about to abandon a purchase.
- **Dynamic Product Ads (DPA)**: Facebook ads that automatically promote products to people who have visited your website or app.
- **Initiate Checkout (IC)**: The number of Initiate Checkout events tracked by the Facebook Pixel attributed to your ads.
- **Landing Page (LP)**: A dedicated page on your website where people who click on your ads are sent.
- **Landing Page Views (LPV)**: The number of times your landing page was viewed by someone who clicked on your ad.
- **Lookalike Audience (LAL / LAA / LLA)**: An audience created from an existing Custom Audience, consisting of people similar to those in the Custom Audience.
- **Middle of Funnel (MOFU)**: The middle stage of the sales process where the product/service is presented as a solution.
- **One-click Upsell (OCU)**: An upsell offer where additional items can be added to the cart with a single click.
- **One-time Offer (OTO)**: An upsell offer for additional items, but for a limited time.
- **Page Post Engagement (PPE)**: A campaign objective to find people most likely to engage with your posts.
- **Retargeting (RT)**: Advertising to people who have already interacted with your content.
- **Return on Investment (ROI)**: The overall return on the money, time, and resources invested in Facebook advertising.
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**: Measures the performance of an advertising campaign. Calculated by dividing total revenue generated by the campaign by the amount spent on the campaign.
- **Top of Funnel (TOFU)**: The first stage of the sales process, used to educate the customer on a problem.
- **Up-sell (US)**: A sales technique to encourage a customer to purchase a more expensive product.
- **Video Views (VV)**: The number of times your Facebook video ad was viewed for three seconds or more.
- **View Content (VC)**: A Facebook advertising campaign optimization to drive traffic to a specific landing page.
- **Website Conversion (WC)**: A campaign objective to find people most likely to convert. Requires Facebook Pixel installation.




## Key Steps for Setting Up a Facebook Ad Campaign

Based on the research, the general steps involved in setting up a Facebook Ad campaign are:

1.  **Create a Facebook Ads Manager Account**: This is the primary platform for managing Facebook ad campaigns.
2.  **Choose Your Campaign Objective**: Select the goal for your ad (e.g., brand awareness, traffic, leads, sales).
3.  **Define Your Audience**: Specify demographics, interests, behaviors, and custom/lookalike audiences.
4.  **Set Your Budget and Schedule**: Determine how much you want to spend and for how long your ad will run.
5.  **Choose Ad Placements**: Decide where your ads will appear (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network).
6.  **Create Your Ad Creative**: Design the visual and textual content of your ad (images, videos, headlines, copy, call-to-action).
7.  **Review and Publish**: Double-check all settings and launch your campaign.
8.  **Monitor and Optimize**: Track performance and make adjustments to improve results.

## Facebook Ad Best Practices

### Creative Best Practices
- **Keep Ad Copy Short**: Primary text should be 1-3 lines at most. Communicate your message concisely [1].
- **Clear Call to Action (CTA)**: Clearly state what you want people to do after seeing your ad. Test different CTA buttons to find what works best [2].
- **High-Quality Visuals**: Use engaging images and videos that are relevant to your audience and message. They don't require expensive equipment [3].
- **Be Authentic**: Authenticity resonates with audiences. Ensure your creatives reflect your brand's true voice and values [4].
- **Know Your Market/Audience**: Tailor your creative content to the specific interests and preferences of your target audience [4].
- **Test Multiple Creatives**: Don't rely on a single ad creative. A/B test different visuals, headlines, and ad copy to see what performs best [5].

### Targeting Best Practices
- **Detailed Targeting Audiences**: Utilize Facebook's detailed targeting options based on demographics, interests, and behaviors [6].
- **Custom Audiences**: Create audiences from your existing customer data (e.g., email lists, website visitors) [6].
- **Lookalike Audiences**: Leverage Custom Audiences to create Lookalike Audiences, which find new people similar to your existing customers or engaged users [6].
- **Separate Audience Funnels by Campaign**: Structure your campaigns to target different stages of the customer journey (e.g., cold, warm, hot audiences) [6].
- **Avoid Over-targeting**: While detailed targeting is powerful, avoid adding too many criteria, as this can limit your reach and increase costs [7].
- **Use Broad Targeting for Awareness**: For top-of-funnel campaigns, consider broader targeting options like location and demographics to maximize reach [7].
- **Leverage Facebook Pixel Data**: Install the Facebook Pixel on your website to track user actions and build more effective Custom and Lookalike Audiences for retargeting and conversion campaigns [8].

### Budgeting Best Practices
- **Allocate Enough Budget for Optimization Event**: Ensure your budget is sufficient for Facebook to optimize for your chosen campaign objective (e.g., conversions, link clicks) [9].
- **Consider Daily vs. Lifetime Budget**: Daily budgets spend a set amount per day, while lifetime budgets spend the amount over the campaign's duration. Choose based on your campaign goals and flexibility needs [10].
- **Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO)**: Allow Facebook to automatically distribute your budget across ad sets to get the best results. This is often recommended for efficiency [10].
- **Start with a Reasonable Budget**: While you can succeed with a small budget, ensure it's enough to gather meaningful data. Some suggest 5%-15% of revenue as a starting point [11].
- **Monitor and Adjust**: Regularly review your ad spend and performance. Be prepared to adjust your budget based on what's working and what's not [12].
