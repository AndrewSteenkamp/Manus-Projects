_# Setting Up Nintendo Switch Gameplay Recording

This guide provides instructions for setting up your Nintendo Switch (and future Switch 2) for high-quality gameplay recording using a capture card and OBS Studio.

## 1. Required Hardware and Software

*   **Nintendo Switch or Switch 2:** With its dock.
*   **HDMI Capture Card:** A quality capture card is essential. Popular and reliable options include:
    *   Elgato HD60 S+
    *   AVerMedia Live Gamer Portable 2 Plus
    *   Elgato 4K60 Pro (for higher-end recording and future-proofing for Switch 2)
*   **PC or Mac:** A computer with sufficient processing power for video recording. Check the specifications of your chosen capture card for minimum requirements.
*   **OBS Studio:** Free and open-source software for video recording and live streaming. Download it from [https://obsproject.com/](https://obsproject.com/).
*   **HDMI Cables:** You will need at least two HDMI cables.

## 2. Connection Setup

1.  **Connect your Nintendo Switch to the capture card:**
    *   Place your Nintendo Switch in its dock.
    *   Connect an HDMI cable from the **HDMI OUT** port on the Switch dock to the **HDMI IN** port on your capture card.
2.  **Connect the capture card to your TV/monitor and computer:**
    *   Connect an HDMI cable from the **HDMI OUT** port on your capture card to your TV or monitor. This is for passthrough, allowing you to play your games without latency.
    *   Connect the capture card to your computer via its USB or PCIe connection.

## 3. OBS Studio Configuration

1.  **Add a Video Capture Device source:**
    *   Open OBS Studio.
    *   In the "Sources" panel, click the **+** button and select "Video Capture Device."
    *   Give the source a name (e.g., "Nintendo Switch").
    *   In the properties window, select your capture card from the "Device" dropdown menu.
    *   You should now see your Nintendo Switch's screen in the OBS preview window.
2.  **Configure Audio:**
    *   In the "Audio Mixer" panel, you should see an audio meter for your "Video Capture Device" source. This is the game audio.
    *   To add commentary, connect a microphone to your computer and add an "Audio Input Capture" source in OBS.
3.  **Recording Settings:**
    *   Go to "File" > "Settings" > "Output."
    *   Set the "Output Mode" to "Advanced."
    *   Under the "Recording" tab:
        *   **Recording Path:** Choose where you want to save your recorded videos.
        *   **Recording Format:** Select a suitable format (e.g., MP4, MKV).
        *   **Encoder:** Choose a hardware encoder (e.g., NVENC for NVIDIA GPUs, AMF for AMD GPUs) if available for better performance. Otherwise, use x264.
        *   **Rate Control:** Set to "CQP" or "CRF" for high-quality recordings. A value of 18-22 is a good starting point (lower is higher quality).

## 4. Start Recording

Once everything is configured, you can start recording your gameplay by clicking the "Start Recording" button in OBS Studio. Your recordings will be saved to the path you specified in the settings.

## 5. Tips for Success

*   **Test your setup:** Before a long recording session, do a short test recording to ensure everything is working correctly (video, audio, quality).
*   **Monitor your PC's performance:** Keep an eye on your PC's CPU and GPU usage to avoid performance issues.
*   **Organize your recordings:** Create a consistent file naming and folder structure to keep your recordings organized.
