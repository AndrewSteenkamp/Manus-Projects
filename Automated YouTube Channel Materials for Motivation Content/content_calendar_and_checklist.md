## YouTube Upload Checklist

This checklist should be used for every video upload to ensure consistency and quality.

**Pre-Upload:**

*   [ ] Final video edited and rendered in 1080p or 4K
*   [ ] Compelling thumbnail created (1280x720 pixels)
*   [ ] SEO-optimized title ready
*   [ ] Detailed video description with timestamps and links ready
*   [ ] Relevant tags identified

**Upload Process:**

*   [ ] Upload video to YouTube
*   [ ] Add title, description, and tags
*   [ ] Upload custom thumbnail
*   [ ] Add video to relevant playlist
*   [ ] Add end screens and cards to promote other videos and encourage subscription
*   [ ] Set video to “Public” and schedule for the designated time

**Post-Upload:**

*   [ ] Share the video on all relevant social media platforms
*   [ ] Engage with comments and feedback from the community
*   [ ] Monitor video analytics to track performance and identify areas for improvement
