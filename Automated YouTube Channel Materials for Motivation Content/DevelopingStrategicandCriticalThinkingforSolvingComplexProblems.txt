Developing Strategic and Critical Thinking for Solving Complex Problems

Align innovation with ethics.
Propose a breakthrough innovation in [industry], ensuring it aligns with ethical standards and addresses unintended consequences.

 

Analyze dilemmas through ethical frameworks.
You are faced with the following ethical dilemma: [describe dilemma]. How would you resolve it? Justify your approach by referencing ethical principles or frameworks.

 

Analyze systems as interrelated parts.
Apply systems thinking to explain the interdependencies within [complex system or issue]. Highlight feedback loops and leverage points.

 

Apply behavioral insights to real decisions.
Apply behavioral economics principles (e.g., nudging, loss aversion) to improve decision-making in [area].

 

Apply this to forecast and manage risks systematically.
Identify potential risks involved in [defined decision or project]. Categorize them by likelihood and impact, and recommend mitigation strategies with rational prioritization.

 

Audit decision-making through an ethical lens.
Design an ethical audit framework for assessing the decision-making processes within [organization or project].

 

Blend knowledge across disciplines.
How can knowledge from [discipline A] and [discipline B] be combined to solve [specific multidimensional challenge]? Provide a concrete example or framework.

 

Break complex problems into manageable units.
Break down the complex problem of [insert problem] into manageable components. Describe a structured plan to address each part step-by-step.

 

Build logical arguments for both sides of an issue.
Prepare structured arguments both in favor of and against [controversial issue]. Ensure clarity, logical consistency, and awareness of counterarguments.

 

Compare costs and benefits for sound judgment.
Perform a detailed cost-benefit analysis of [option or strategy], considering short-term vs. long-term trade-offs and qualitative vs. quantitative factors.

 

Compare strategies side-by-side.
Compare and contrast two alternative approaches to [challenge]. Evaluate each based on feasibility, risk, and long-term value.

 

Connect theory to practice.
Use [specific theory or model] to explain or predict outcomes in [real-world situation]. Clarify how the theory maps to the elements of the scenario.

 

Constructively evaluate a peer’s solution.
Review and critique a peer’s proposed solution to [specific challenge]. Offer constructive feedback and suggest a refined or alternative approach.

 

Contrast leadership styles in practice.
Compare two leadership styles (e.g., transformational vs. transactional) and evaluate which is more effective in [defined situation].

 

Design scalable and functional systems.
Design an efficient and scalable system to manage [specific process or task]. Outline key components, workflows, and how it addresses core pain points.

 

Diagnose root causes of persistent issues.
Use root cause analysis (e.g., "5 Whys", Ishikawa diagram) to identify the underlying cause of [persistent problem]. Suggest actionable next steps.

 

Draft thoughtful public or private sector policies.
Recommend a practical, evidence-based policy to address [systemic issue]. Describe expected benefits, potential drawbacks, and implementation considerations.

 

Evaluate broader consequences of new developments.
Evaluate the short- and long-term impact of [innovation, disruption, or policy] on [target industry, group, or environment]. Consider both intended and unintended effects.

 

Evaluate long-term consequences of actions or policies.
Analyze the potential long-term consequences of [policy, decision, or innovation] on [specific sector or population]. Address economic, social, and environmental dimensions.

 

Evaluate scenarios using game theory.
Analyze [competitive or cooperative situation] using principles of game theory. Identify dominant strategies and likely outcomes.

Based on current trends and data, predict the future development of [industry, technology, or behavior]. Justify your forecast with assumptions and references.

Great for idea generation and out-of-the-box thinking.
Propose a novel, creative solution to [widespread challenge]. Explain how it improves upon existing approaches and assess its feasibility and scalability.

 

Guide organizational change with strategy.
Design a change management plan to support the implementation of [organizational or strategic shift]. Include communication, resistance handling, and success tracking.

 

Helps critically assess an existing approach to highlight improvements.
Critically assess the effectiveness of [specific method or approach] within [given scenario]. Analyze strengths, weaknesses, assumptions, and provide reasoned alternatives.

 

Ideal for outlining detailed, multi-stakeholder strategies with measurable goals.
Develop a comprehensive strategic plan to address [defined issue] in [particular context]. Include clear objectives, key milestones, involved stakeholders, risks, and measurable success criteria.

 

Identify and counter cognitive bias.
Identify potential cognitive or systemic biases in the analysis or decision-making process for [specific situation]. Suggest techniques to minimize them.

 

Justify major strategic pivots.
Justify a strategic pivot for an organization facing [external pressure or internal failure]. Present data-driven reasoning.

 

Lead through crisis simulation.
You are in charge during a crisis: [brief description]. How do you stabilize the situation, communicate with stakeholders, and ensure long-term recovery?

 

Learn from failure systematically.
Conduct a failure analysis for [project or initiative that failed]. Identify lessons learned and how they can guide future action.

 

Leverage AI for better decisions.
How can AI be ethically and effectively used to support decision-making in [domain]? Address benefits, risks, and oversight mechanisms.

 

Make sound decisions under uncertainty.
In a high-uncertainty context such as [describe], describe tools and principles for making resilient decisions.

 

Map influence and interest of key players.
Create a stakeholder map for [initiative or change process]. Identify their interests, influence, and the best engagement strategy.

 

Measure complex systems wisely.
Design appropriate metrics to monitor and evaluate the success of [complex system or initiative]. Ensure they capture meaningful insights.

 

Negotiate under complexity.
You must negotiate a multi-stakeholder agreement on [issue]. How would you align diverging interests and reach a compromise?

 

Practice leadership in high-stakes situations.
As a leader facing [specific crisis or challenge], describe how you would navigate it. Address communication, decision-making, and stakeholder management.

 

Prepare for multiple future states.
Develop three future scenarios for [context: e.g., a city's infrastructure in 2040] and explain how an organization could prepare for each.

 

Prioritize and justify resource use.
Devise a resource allocation strategy for [defined scenario] to maximize impact and efficiency. Justify your priorities and trade-offs.

 

Prioritize decisions using matrix logic.
Use a decision-priority matrix (e.g., Eisenhower, Impact/Effort) to rank and justify competing tasks or options in [real project].

 

Provide critical review of methodologies or frameworks.
Write a balanced critical review of [specific methodology, policy, or model] used in [industry or domain]. Include supporting data or examples.

 

Reflect deeply on learning from experience.
Reflect on a real decision or experience you’ve had that involved complexity. What would you do differently based on what you’ve learned since?

 

Spot flawed logic and correct it.
Identify common heuristics or logical fallacies that may distort thinking in [scenario]. Suggest corrections.

 

Use for structured analysis of complex future situations.
Given this scenario — [describe scenario] — identify key variables, underlying issues, and possible outcomes. Recommend the most effective response strategy.

 

Useful for building structured hypothesis testing plans.
Formulate a testable hypothesis regarding [specific phenomenon]. Design a valid research approach with variables, data sources, and expected outcomes to confirm or reject the hypothesis.

 

Use mental models to think clearly.
Apply mental models (e.g., first principles, inversion, second-order thinking) to reason through [specific decision].

 

Use this to structure a logical, justified solution to a real-world issue.
Propose a clear, step-by-step solution to [specific, real-world problem], leveraging your expertise in [relevant domain or framework]. Justify each step with logical reasoning or supporting evidence.

 

View an issue from two opposing lenses.
Explore [topic or issue] from two fundamentally different perspectives. Analyze how each viewpoint influences the interpretation of facts and the resulting conclusions.

 

Weigh tough trade-offs.
What trade-offs would you accept in [scenario: e.g., product design, public health policy]? Justify your rationale.
